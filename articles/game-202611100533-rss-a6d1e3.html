"YoshiP Radio: YoshiP Night," hosted by Shuhei Yoshida, will be held in Shibuya on November 29. The event will feature yona, the developer of "DareKare," and Yuki Katayama of Kodansha Game Lab, as they discuss the journey of indie game development and offer a behind-the-scenes look at the production and support processes from the perspectives of creators and editors.

Details (EN)

CD PROJEKT announced today that, in conjunction with the release of the remastered update for the open-world RPG *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* on September 29, the Xbox version will be compatible with "Xbox Play Anywhere." Players who own the Xbox version will be able to claim the PC version from the Microsoft Store at no additional cost.

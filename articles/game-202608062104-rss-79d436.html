Details (EN)

Nintendo today announced its financial results for the first quarter of the fiscal year ending March 2027. Although sales decreased 9.5% year-on-year to 517.8 billion yen, operating income increased 150.5% year-on-year to 142.5 billion yen due to factors such as tax refunds based on the US IEEPA. Nintendo Switch 2 hardware sales were 3.82 million units, a 34.4% decrease from the previous year.

Details (EN)

The CEDEC+KYUSHU2026 Organizing Committee has released the full session details and schedule for “CEDEC+KYUSHU2026,” which will be held on November 14, 2026, at Building 1 of Kyushu Sangyo University. A total of 35 sessions are scheduled for this conference. The keynote speaker will be Akihiro Hino of Level-5, and the special guest speaker will be Hitoshi Fujisawa of Story Note.

Indie development team PopElephantStudio has started distributing the trial version of their new title ``My Demolition History'' on Steam. This work is a psychological horror adventure that depicts the twisted codependence of the exiled prince Hector, who has lost everything, and his uncle and lieutenant Matvey, who supports him. By examining the everyday items in the room, Hector dismantles the memories and ego that he has rejected.

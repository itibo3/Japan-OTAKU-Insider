Details (EN)

A commercial for Ezaki Glico’s frozen smoothie “PAPICO,” featuring voice actor Akio Otsuka—known for roles such as Black Jack in the “Black Jack” series and Daisuke Jigen in “Lupin the Third”—has been airing since October 27, 2026. The commercial, which makes full use of Otsuka’s deep, gravelly voice, has drawn attention from voice acting fans, including fellow voice actor Hiroki Yasumoto, who shared his reaction on his X (formerly Twitter) account.

Details (EN)

It has been announced that the collaboration event ``Anime ``Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba'' where you can experience the world view of the anime ``Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba'' will be held from June 12th to July 26th, 2026 at the hands-on retail facility ``Bandai Namco Cross Store Tokyo'' in Ikebukuro, Tokyo. Ufotable's original illustrations with the theme of "reminiscence" will appear, and after Tokyo, it will be held in Osaka, Hakata, and Yokohama.

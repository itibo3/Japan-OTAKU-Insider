Yostar's mahjong game ``Jantama'' will be holding a collaboration tournament ``Washisu Mahjong Showdown'' with the manga ``Akagi.'' The two winners of the online preliminary round will team up with a professional mahjong player in the offline final, playing under special rules that make many of the tiles transparent. Qualifying starts on June 12, 2026.

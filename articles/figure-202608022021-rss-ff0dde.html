Details (EN)

1/7 scale figure "Yuki Mizukami" scheduled to be released in February 2027 from MAGI ARTS. This is a three-dimensional version of Yuki Mizukami, who appears in the adventure game "Wonderful Days ~Discontinuous Existence~" created by KeroQ. In addition to the regular version, this product comes in two types: a deluxe version that comes with replacement face parts and an original illustration tapestry.

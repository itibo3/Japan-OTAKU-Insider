Details (EN)

The TV anime *Hirayasumi* will begin airing on NHK General TV in January 2027. The main visual and teaser PV for the series have now been unveiled. This PV marks the first public reveal of the anime footage and character voices, and it was also announced that Yuma Uchida will voice the protagonist, Hiroto Ikuta; Mayu Takahashi will voice Natsumi Kobayashi; and Rikako Aikawa will voice Hanae Wada.

Details (EN)

iamleyathankyou released the store page for the new PC software ``K-jihacheol'' on August 8, 2026. This work is a typing game based on subway and urban railway lines around the world. It includes a total of 2,361 stations in 26 cities in 19 countries, including 14 lines in Tokyo and 8 lines in Osaka. However, input must be in the local language.

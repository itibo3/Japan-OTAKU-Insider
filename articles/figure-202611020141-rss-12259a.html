Details (EN)

From *Five Star Stories*, the “Imperial Cavalry Magna Palace: The Knight of Gold”—the flagship symbol of the Amaterasu Dynasty—is now available as a 1/144-scale plastic model kit from Awa Treasure. In this article, we present a fully painted model that highlights the details using five metallic colors while bringing out the full brilliance of the “Magna Palace Gold” specialty paint by Gaia Notes, which was released simultaneously.

Details (EN)

Volks is currently accepting pre-orders for the plastic model kits “IMS 1/144 Bash the Black Knight (Reissue),” “IMS 1/100 The Night of Gold (Reissue),” and “IMS 1/100 L.E.D. Mirage V3 Light Armor Version (Reissue).” Pre-orders will be accepted until today, October 12. Prices are 5,280 yen for the “IMS 1/144 Bash the Black Knight (Reissue),” 11,000 yen for the “IMS 1/100 The Night of Gold (Reissue),” and and “IMS 1/100 LED Mirage V3 Light Armament Version (Reissue)” is 13,200 yen.

At the Phoenixx booth at TGS 2026, we interviewed Yudai Nozaki of Phoenixx about “THEFIRSTZOMBIE.” Produced by Kentaro Fujii, this game lets players control a protagonist who has become a zombie and turn humans into zombies to expand their group. We asked him about how the concept came about, the development process, and even the behind-the-scenes story of creating the game’s pop-style characters.

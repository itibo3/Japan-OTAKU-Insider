KADOKAWA's classic romance manga label "Comic Aily" released four comics simultaneously on June 4, 2026. To commemorate this occasion, the voice comic PVs for ``Love Guidance for Subscription Handsome Men I Have Never Heard of Living Together'' and ``Two People in Love Hide Their Fangs - A Story of a Couple with Too Much Gap'' starring voice actors Nobuhiko Okamoto, Toshiki Masuda, and Himena Naota have been released.

Pre-orders for the 2nd photo book ``Ortensia'' by Hikaru Kabashima, an actor who made a sudden breakthrough with his role as Aguilera/Natsuki Hana in ``Kamen Rider Revise'' and has a strong presence in many fields, have recently started accepting reservations on various sales sites. This book is about her once-in-a-lifetime photo shoot on Venice's Lido Island, which she has always admired, and also includes the long-awaited swimsuit shot for the first time in about two years.

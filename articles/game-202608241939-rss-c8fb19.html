Atlus announced on August 24, 2026 that ``Persona 4 Revival'' and the Nintendo Switch 2 version ``Metaphor: Refantasio'' will be exhibited as playable games at the Tokyo Game Show 2026. In the former, you can experience either ``Story'' or ``Dungeon,'' and in the latter, you can experience ``Trial of the Sword,'' an experiential game content based on the battle system.

Details (EN)

The animated films *Panda! Panda!* and its sequel, *Panda! Panda! The Rainy Circus*, are about to mark the 55th anniversary of their theatrical releases. To commemorate this occasion, it has been announced that the “55th Anniversary Project” will launch on October 1, 2026. The project will run until 2028, the 55th anniversary of “The Rainy Day Circus Chapter.” As the first phase, the anniversary logo has been unveiled, and details regarding collaborations and events have been announced.

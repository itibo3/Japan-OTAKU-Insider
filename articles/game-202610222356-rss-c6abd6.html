On October 21, 2026, the VideoGameHistoryFoundation (VGHF), a U.S. nonprofit organization dedicated to preserving and researching the history of video games, made the “Colin Williamson Photography Collection” available in its online archive. More than 300 photographs capturing Japanese gaming events and cityscapes from the late 1990s through the 2000s are available for free viewing.

Details (EN)

A preview screening of the completed anime ``New Theatrical Version ☆ Sergeant Keroro: Resurrection of the Earth is in Crisis of Extinction!'' based on the original work by Kannon Yoshizaki was held today, June 3rd, at Nissho Hall in Tokyo. Before the screening, a stage greeting was held, with Kumiko Watanabe who played Sergeant Keroro, Jesse (SixTONES) who played Arles/Dells, Shinobu Hasegawa (Sissonne) who was in charge of the narration, and Yuichi Fukuda, the scriptwriter and general director, taking the stage. A costume of Sergeant Keroro also rushed in.

Details (EN)

It has been revealed that the total number of attendees at the “Tokyo Game Show 2026,” which concluded its in-person event today, was 257,642. Based on figures from the four-day period from September 17 to 20, this represents a decrease of 5,459 people compared to the previous year’s total of 263,101. Although the event was originally scheduled to run for five days, the September 21 session was canceled due to Typhoon No. 25. The 2027 event is scheduled to take place at Makuhari Messe from September 16 to 20.

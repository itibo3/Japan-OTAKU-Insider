Details (EN)

Wizards of the Coast will release a new set for the trading card game (TCG) Magic: The Gathering (Magic) on June 26th, ``Magic: The Gathering | Marvel Super Heroes'' (hereinafter Marvel Super Heroes). As the name suggests, ``Marvel Super Heroes'' is a special new set created in collaboration with the Marvel Universe. Magic has released collaboration sets with various popular contents, and the collaboration with ``FINAL FANTASY'' released in June 2025 was a huge hit.

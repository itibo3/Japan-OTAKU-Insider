Details (EN)

Megapop and KasedoGames today announced a partnership with the marine conservation organization "WhaleandDolphin Conservation" for the underwater city construction sim "Life Below" scheduled to be released on Steam on May 26, 2026. Supporter packs that add special visiting creatures such as bottlenose dolphins, spotted dolphins, and porpoises to the game will be distributed on the same day of release.

Details (EN)

Playstack today released “Dicevaders,” developed by Pengonauts, for PC, Switch 2, and Switch. In this deck-building roguelike, players take on the role of an alien commander, using dice to assemble an army and invade Earth. Players place units on a grid, roll dice, and execute combos to increase their attack score.

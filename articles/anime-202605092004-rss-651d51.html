Details (EN)

A special drama about Osamu Tezuka, "Osamu Tezuka's War," produced by NHK Osaka Broadcasting Station, will be broadcast in August. Kengo Kora will play the role of Osamu Tezuka, and Kounosuke Harada will play the role of Tetsuro Okan, the main character of Tezuka's work ``Paper Fortress.'' On May 7th, a production presentation was held at Takarazuka Osamu Tezuka Memorial Museum in Hyogo, with cast members Takara and Harada and producer Akihiro Tajima in attendance.

Details (EN)

Merchandise featuring a newly drawn illustration with a “black suit” theme from the TV anime *Yu Yu Hakusho* is now available. It has been confirmed that these items will be available for pre-sale at the “Chugai Mining Booth” during “Jump Festa 2027,” to be held on December 19 and 20, 2026, at the Makuhari Messe International Exhibition Hall in Chiba. Pre-orders will also be accepted on “Chugaionline,” the e-commerce site operated by Chugai Mining’s Content Division.

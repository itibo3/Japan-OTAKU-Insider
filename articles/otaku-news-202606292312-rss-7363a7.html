Details (EN)

The final rankings of the Sanrio character popularity voting event ``2026 Sanrio Character Award'' have been announced. The total number of votes was 70,647,379, a record 112% compared to last year, and ``Pom Pom Purin'' took first place. In its 30th anniversary year, it collected more than 7 million votes and won the title for the second consecutive year and fifth time in total.

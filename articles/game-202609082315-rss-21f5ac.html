Mebius announced today that it will release the PC version of “Spiral Lady: Makina with Super Hair,” developed by Silver Star Japan, on Steam in late September. This action game features “Kaminaga Makina,” a young lady with blonde, vertical curls, as the protagonist. Players must make full use of her ability to manipulate her hair to reach the goal located somewhere within each stage.

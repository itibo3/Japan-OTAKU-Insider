Details (EN)

It has been revealed that a collaboration project to commemorate the 5th anniversary of the TV anime "Jujutsu Kaisen" will be held at the conveyor belt sushi chain "Kura Sushi" stores nationwide from May 15, 2026. Collaboration menus with images of Yuji Kojo, Megumi Fushiguro, Bara Kugisaki, Kento Nanami, and Satoru Gojo will be available for the first time, as well as goods exclusively drawn by Kura Sushi.

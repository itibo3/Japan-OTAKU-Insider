Details (EN)

TV anime “Iron Pot Jean!” ' will be broadcast on 6 TV Tokyo network stations from July 5, 2026. The main PV (back version), which condenses the dangerous charm of this work, and the key visual that foretells a battle between passionate chefs have been released. Furthermore, it was announced that Tomokazu Sugita will be playing the role of Ryuji Bito, the user of XO sauce, as an additional cast member, and the ending theme song has been decided to be Emuni Mini's ``Cried Devil''.

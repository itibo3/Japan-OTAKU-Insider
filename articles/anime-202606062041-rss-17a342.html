Details (EN)

The TV anime "Osomatsu-san" 10th anniversary special event "10th ANNIVERSARY Osomatsu-san SPECIAL 6DAYS Opening Festival" was held today, June 6th. The event held at Marunouchi Piccadilly in Tokyo included Takahiro Sakurai, who plays Osomatsu, Yuichi Nakamura, who plays Karamatsu, Hiroshi Kamiya, who plays Choromatsu, Jun Fukuyama, who plays Ichimatsu, and Kou, who plays Jushimatsu. Daisuke No, Miyu Irino who plays Todomatsu, Aya Endo who plays Totoko, Yuki Kuniri who plays Chibita, Yoji Ueda who plays Dekapan, Nobuo Tobita who plays Dayon, and Momoko Saito who plays Hatabo took the stage.

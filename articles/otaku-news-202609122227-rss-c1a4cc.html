Details (EN)

It has been announced that the TV anime *Breath* will air nationwide on Fuji TV’s “Noitamina” block starting in January 2027. A teaser visual and a character promotional video featuring scenes from the series have been released, and it has been confirmed that Tomotaka Ohno will voice the protagonist, Aia Udagawa, and Chiyuki Miura will voice Jun Sumizaki.

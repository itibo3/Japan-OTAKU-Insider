Summary

Overseas hobby manufacturer ``HASUKI'' will release the action figure ``Pocket Art Dolls Frontline 2: Excilium PA013 Kurukai Aoki Kiseki Ver.'' in May 2027. The price is 14,300 yen for the regular version and 19,250 yen for the deluxe version.

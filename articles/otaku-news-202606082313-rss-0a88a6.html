Details (EN)

The 29th installment of the Detective Conan movie series, Detective Conan: Fallen Angel on the Highway, is currently in theaters. It has recently been revealed that the box office revenue of this work has exceeded 13.03 billion yen as of June 7, 2026. The film has risen to 32nd in the all-time box office revenue ranking according to Kogyo Tsushinsha, and is expected to surpass the previous film Detective Conan Submarine (13.88 billion yen), which was released in 2023.

Details (EN)

Tokyo Marui will hold a test shooting event for the gas blowback machine gun "Type 20 5.56mm Rifle" on June 21st at the BRAVE POINT Shinjuku store. Participation will be by lottery, and the application period is until 23:59 on June 14th. You can apply from the lottery application page below. A Google account is required to apply. It will also be held at BLKFOX Fussa on June 28th.

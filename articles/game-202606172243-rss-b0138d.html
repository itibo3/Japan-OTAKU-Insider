Details (EN)

Hamster today announced that distribution of ``Console Archives Legend of Shadows'' will begin on June 18, 2026. ``The Legend of Shadows'' is an ACT game released by Taito in 1986 for 8-bit home game consoles. The player becomes the ``shadow'' of an Iga ninja and fights in a demonic country to save ``Kirihime'' who has been kidnapped by a demonic being.

It has been announced that the "Gundam ZERO," the main mobile suit from the "RG project" animated series *Mobile Suit Gundam RG XARX-ZERO (Alex Zero)*—created by the duo of Kenji Kamiyama and Toh Enjo—will be released as part of the "HG" series. A prototype of the model is currently on display at Gundam Base Tokyo.

Details (EN)

Rainbowholic's hands-on stationery store ``Rainbowholic Stationery Land'' opened on August 15, 2026, a 2-minute walk from the east exit of Komagome Station on the JR Yamanote Line. ``Rainbowholic Stationery Cafe'' in Jujo, Tokyo has relocated and changed its name, and as a ``stationery theme park'' it will feature over 300 types of limited edition stickers, 6 themed spots, and a free stationery experience corner.

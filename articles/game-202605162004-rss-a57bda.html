Polyarc announced on May 16, 2026 that it will release the adventure game "Moss: The Forgotten Relic" this summer. This title combines "Moss: Volume 1" and "Moss: Volume 2" into one book. The player guides Quill, a small but brave mouse, as he explores a ruined kingdom that is being swallowed up by nature.

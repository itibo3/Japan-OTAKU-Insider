The esports team REJECT announced that it will serve a collaborative ramen dish—created in partnership with team streamer Amaki Pururu, professional gamer Fudo, and the ramen shop “Karashibi Miso Ramen Onikinbō”—at Onikinbō Lab in Tokyo. The first round will run from September 18 to 20, 2026, and the second round from September 25 to 27.

Bell Fine is releasing a 1/7 scale figure of Arisu Sakayanagi scheduled to be released in March 2027. This is a three-dimensional version of Arisu Sakayanagi, the genius girl who appears in the TV anime "Welcome to the Classroom of Ability." This time, the figure is sitting on an original pedestal with a chessboard motif, making it an impressive work.

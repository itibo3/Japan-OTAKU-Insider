Pre-orders for "CSM 15 Hero Ghost Eye Soul Set" from "Kamen Rider Ghost" will start from 16:00 on July 6th at Preban.

Published: 2026-07-06
ID: figure-202607062252-rss-436bcd
Category: figure

Japanese title

「仮面ライダーゴースト」より「CSM 15英雄ゴースト眼魂セット」がプレバンにて7月6日16時より予約開始

Summary

Bandai will begin accepting pre-orders for the adult role-playing toy ``CSM 15 Hero Ghost Eye Soul Set'' on the mail order site ``Premium Bandai'' from 4:00 pm on July 6th. Shipping is scheduled for December, and the price is 16,500 yen.

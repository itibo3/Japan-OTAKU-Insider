Summary

Takara Tomy will release the “Tomica Jobbraver TJBDX DX Skybraver JAL Jet Airliner” in early October. The price is 9,350 yen. Pre-orders will begin on August 27 on the “Takara Tomy Mall” online store and other retailers.

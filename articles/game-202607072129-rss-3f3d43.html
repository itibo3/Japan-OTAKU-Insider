Individual developer Isui announced today (July 7, 2026) that he will release the Steam version of Touhou Project's derivative game ``Kagiyayama Hina and the others who want to relieve their body anxiety with Yokai Dog!!'' on July 17th. This is a word play puzzle simulation game in which you rewrite the diagnostic results by changing or adding the characters presented.

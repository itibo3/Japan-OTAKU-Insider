Details (EN)

Ken Elephant will release a new capsule toy ``Kaigen Goods Collection'' in collaboration with Kaigen Pharma's cold medicine ``Kaigen'' in late September. There are 5 types in total, each priced at 400 yen. In addition to capsule toy stores nationwide, it will be sold at hobby shops, online shops, some bookstores and miscellaneous goods stores, some LOFT stores, and Kenele Stand stores.

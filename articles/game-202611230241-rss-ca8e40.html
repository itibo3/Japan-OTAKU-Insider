Details (EN)

The adventure game for women, *Goddess Absent*, is being showcased as a playable title at “WePlayExpo 2026,” taking place in Shanghai, China, on November 21 and 22, 2026. In this game, players take on the role of a goddess who has reappeared before the people for the first time in hundreds of years. At the event, attendees were able to experience the opening of the story—where the plot unfolds differently depending on whose prayers the goddess chooses to heed—as well as card battles in which players must strike a balance between faith in the goddess and resentment toward her.

The “Godzilla Backpack Series,” a collaboration between the “Godzilla” franchise and “Dinosaur Base,” a specialty store for dinosaur-themed backpacks in Fukui Prefecture, is set to debut. The series features three designs—Godzilla, King Ghidorah, and Mechagodzilla—and pre-orders will begin on January 23, 2027.

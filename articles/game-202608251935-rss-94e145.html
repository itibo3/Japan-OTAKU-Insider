Details (EN)

TMXNGames today launched a limited-time beta test on Steam for “AmongShadows,” a game that combines the Werewolf game with a retreat-style action RPG. In this game, players are divided into the “Order of the Secret Flame,” which aims to eliminate the source of the infection, and the “Priests of Darkness” lurking within the order, engaging in sword-and-sorcery combat and identity deduction. The test runs until 6:00 PM on August 31.

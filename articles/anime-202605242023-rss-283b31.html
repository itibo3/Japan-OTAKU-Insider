An advance screening of the TV anime ``My Younger Brothers Are Excuse'' based on the original work by Akira Ozaki was held today, May 24th, at United Cinema Aqua City Odaiba in Tokyo. In this article, we will report on the talk show held before the screening with Naomi Ozora, who plays Ito Narita, Toshiki Masuda, who plays Gen Narita, Taku Yashiro, who plays Raku Narita, Kensho Ono, who plays Hiragi Narita, and Momoka Terasawa, who plays Rui Narita.

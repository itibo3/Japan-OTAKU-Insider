To commemorate the 2.5th anniversary of the release of the ``Love and Deep Sky'' app, we are collaborating with ``Bloomit'', a print sticker (hereinafter referred to as ``Puri'') machine with approximately 500 units nationwide. For a limited time from July 20th (Monday) to August 31st (Monday), "Bloomit" is equipped with a photography course where you can enjoy the world view of "Love and Deep Sky."

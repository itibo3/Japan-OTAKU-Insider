Details (EN)

The latest issue of Kobunsha’s “FLASH”—a one-of-a-kind weekly visual magazine—featuring Nene Shida, who is active as both a gravure model and an actress (known for her role as Sara Sakurai/Kamen Rider Hakubi in *Kamen Rider Gits*), gracing the cover and opening pages, went on sale on August 25, 2026. In this issue, she appears in her first gravure spread for the magazine in about two years, showcasing her innocent smile and a swimsuit look.

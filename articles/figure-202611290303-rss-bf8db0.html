Details (EN)

BANDAI SPIRITS has announced the release of the articulated figures "METAL ROBOT Soul <SIDE MS> Black Knight Squad Carla" and the "METAL ROBOT Soul <SIDE MS> Black Knight Squad Carla Exclusive Effect Parts Set" until 11:00 PM today, November 29. The price for “METAL ROBOT Soul <SIDE MS> Black Knight Scord Carla” is 26,400 yen, and the price for “METAL ROBOT Soul <SIDE MS> Black Knight Scord Carla Exclusive Effect Parts Set” is 6,050 yen. Shipment is scheduled for April 2027.

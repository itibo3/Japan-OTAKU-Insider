"Mecha Comic" (Mecha-Comi), one of Japan's largest digital comic distribution services, celebrated the 20th anniversary of its launch on November 2, 2026. To mark the occasion, popular creators such as Tsukasa Hojo of *City Hunter* have posted congratulatory messages featuring illustrations on X (formerly Twitter), which are also currently available on the service’s 20th-anniversary website.

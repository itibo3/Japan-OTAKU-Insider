The Steam store page and announcement trailer for “GhostsoftheMountainPass,” a public road battle RPG currently in development by SorpassoGames, have been released. Set in Italy in the late 1990s, the game features a series of one-on-one battles where the health bar of the trailing car decreases as the gap between vehicles widens, and players can upgrade their cars using parts earned from victories.

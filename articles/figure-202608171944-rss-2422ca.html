Kaiyodo will start pre-orders for resale of the movable figure “Revoltech Amazing Yamaguchi Genos” from August 21st!

Published: 2026-08-17
ID: figure-202608171944-rss-2422ca
Category: figure
Source: https://hobby.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2133250.html

Japanese title

海洋堂、可動フィギュア「リボルテック　アメイジング・ヤマグチ　ジェノス」の再販分を8月21日より予約開始！

Summary

Kaiyodo has announced that pre-orders for the resale of the action figure ``Revoltech Amazing Yamaguchi Genos'' will begin from 12:00 on August 21st. The resale date is scheduled for April 2027, and the price is 10,450 yen.

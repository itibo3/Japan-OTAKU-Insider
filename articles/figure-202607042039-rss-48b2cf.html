Details (EN)

As of July 2026, an "abnormal situation" is occurring in Takara Tomy's "Beyblade X." In addition to the bay itself, products such as the stadium and launcher have completely disappeared from store shelves. In response to this situation, Takara Tomy has announced that it will increase production of Beyblades and will hold a lottery sale for new products.

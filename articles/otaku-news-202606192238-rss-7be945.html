Details (EN)

The Kansai touring exhibition ``Ghost and the Shell'', a large-scale exhibition spanning the entire Ghost in the Shell anime series, will be held from July 17th to August 30th, 2026 at the 3rd floor gallery of the Gallery Building of the Hyogo Prefectural Museum of Art. We have recently received information about new goods that will be available from the Kansai traveling exhibition, including new anime products and a new collaboration T-shirt with GEEKS RULE. Furthermore, it has been revealed that interactive AI content ``Tachikoma AI'' will appear at the venue on the opening day.

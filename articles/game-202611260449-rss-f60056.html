Asmodee Digital, the operator of the online board game site “Board Game Arena,” officially launched the economic strategy game “Brass: Lancashire” on November 25, 2026. Players take on the role of industrialists in England during the Industrial Revolution, building industrial facilities and connecting towns via canals and railroads to earn points.

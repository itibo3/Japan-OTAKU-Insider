Details (EN)

Capcom announced the tournament details for the “Street Fighter League ASIA 2027” today. The tournament will be held in the fall of 2027 with a total of six teams, and the single team representing the JAPAN region will be selected through an open tryout tournament. In addition, new information was announced, including the key visual for “CAPCOM CUP 13” and details on the official women’s tournament, the “Queen’s Cup” (tentative title).

Japanese title

「Re:ゼロから始める異世界生活」より「ラム」「レム」がカドプラシリーズで立体化。5月29日より予約開始

Summary

KADOKAWA will release the KADOKAWA PLASTIC MODEL SERIES ``Re:ZERO -Starting Life in Another World'' Ram/Rem in November. The price is 9,680 yen each for the regular version and 12,980 yen each for the DX ver.

株式会社BS日本が「BS日テレ」に社名変更　BS11の「日本BS放送」と紛らわしさ解消へ

Summary

BS Nippon Co., Ltd. announced on the 8th that at the general meeting of shareholders held on June 5th, it was resolved to change its trade name as of July 1st, 2026. The new trade name is BS Nippon Television Corporation.

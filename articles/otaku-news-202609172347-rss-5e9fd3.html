Details (EN)

On September 16, 2026, a premiere screening of *To You Who Are Farther Away Than Anywhere Else*, featuring a stage greeting, was held. Hisa Ishibashi, Anna Nagase, Kamiya Tsuchiya, Hiroshi Tamaki, Tota, and Director Junichi Wada took the stage to share their thoughts on the finished film, their feelings toward the Oki Islands—which served as the inspiration for the story—and behind-the-scenes stories about the creation of the theme song “Olive.”

Details (EN)

Marvelous announced on August 6, 2026 that a special live broadcast commemorating the 30th anniversary of the "Harvest Moon" series and the 20th anniversary of the "Rune Factory" series will be streamed on the company's official YouTube channel and X on August 24th at 20:00. This live broadcast will include a look back at the history of both series, as well as several other events such as announcing the results of character voting.

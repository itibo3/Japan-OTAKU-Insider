The anime *Disney Twisted Wonderland: The Animation ~Episode of Savana Claw~* will be streamed exclusively on Disney’s official video streaming service, “Disney+,” starting December 23, 2026. A new payoff PV showcasing Leona Kingscolor’s dark, burning passion, along with the latest stills from the series, have been released. Additionally, the ending theme song has been confirmed as “DIADEM,” performed by the residents of the Savana Claw dormitory.

"Sanrio Sports," the sportswear and merchandise collection brand featuring Sanrio characters, is launching its Fall/Winter 2026 golf wear and related merchandise. These "cute" yet sophisticated items, suitable not only for the golf course but also for everyday wear, will be released sequentially at the Sanrio Online Shop flagship store and the World Online Store. Additionally, it has been announced that limited-time pop-up stores will be held for the first time in Tokyo, Osaka, and Nagoya starting September 23.

In collaboration with the TV anime ``Hitorigoto of the Pharmacy'', ``Mao Mao's Pharmacy Visit'' is currently being held by ``three advanced Chinese herbal medicine universities'': Yokohama Pharmaceutical University, Nippon Pharmaceutical University, and Daiichi Pharmaceutical University. We have recently begun distributing university guidebooks for each university with the cover featuring an original illustration from this work. It has also been revealed that starting in July, the second round of open campus participant benefits will include a "clear file with a collaboration-exclusive illustration."

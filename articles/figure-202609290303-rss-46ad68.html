Details (EN)

Pre-orders for the “Kōbu Daijinki Model” pocket watch—commemorating the 30th anniversary of *Sakura Wars*—will begin on Tuesday, September 29, at 10:00 a.m., with a limited run of 2,026 pieces! The lid features a three-dimensional engraving of Kōbu, the dial is modeled after a cockpit, and the hands are inspired by the twin swords of the Niten Ichi-ryū style! The included card featuring the six members of the Flower Troupe is another highlight. #SakuraTaisen #SakuraTaisen30thAnniversary

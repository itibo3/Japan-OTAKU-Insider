Details (EN)

D4 Enterprises will release “EGG Console Deep Dungeon II: The Emblem of the Brave (MSX)” on September 17, 2026. This is a re-release of the RPG originally released in 1988. Set several hundred years after the events of the previous game, players take on the role of a descendant of the hero Ral and face off against the revived Demon King Ruu. In addition to the content from the original game, the re-release includes new features such as exploration of the surface world and 2D-rendered towns.

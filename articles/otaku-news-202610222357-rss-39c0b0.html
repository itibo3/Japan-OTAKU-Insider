Details (EN)

It has been announced that Kenshi Yonezu will perform the theme song for the film *The Apothecary’s Tale: The Late Empress’s Treasure*, set for release on December 11, 2026. The latest trailer featuring his new song “Haru ga Akari,” along with the key visual and a character relationship chart, have also been released. Yonezu revealed that he created the song by studying the script and drawing inspiration from children’s songs.

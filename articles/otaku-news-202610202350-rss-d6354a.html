From "Chiikawa," the "Sparkling Miracle Ribbon Charm Gummies" are now available. Each package includes one of 13 different sparkling key-shaped charms, and the gummies are orange-flavored and shaped like Chiikawa, Hachiware, and a rabbit. They will go on sale starting in February 2027 at candy sections in mass-market retailers nationwide.

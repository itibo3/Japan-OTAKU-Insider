Details (EN)

sj@Saka today announced “Welcome Home, Toru,” a PC title currently in development. This first-person horror game puts players in the role of a former watchmaker whose memory is failing as he explores a Showa-era house during a power outage. Players can hold up to six memories at a time, and to recall one, they must let go of another.

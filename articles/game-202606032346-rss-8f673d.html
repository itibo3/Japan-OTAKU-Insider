Details (EN)

Hamster today announced that distribution of ``Console Archives Kantaro Tamanage Kantaro's Fifty-Three Stations of the Tokaido'' will begin distribution on June 4, 2026. ``Tantaro Tanage Kantaro's Fifty-Three Stations of the Tokaido'' is an ACT game released by Sunsoft in 1986. Kantaro, a fireworks craftsman, travels through the Tokaido to set off fireworks at the Sumida River in Edo.

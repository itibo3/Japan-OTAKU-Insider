Japanese title

「ハイスクールD×D」刊行15周年を記念した「姫島朱乃」、「リアス・グレモリー」1/6.5スケールフィギュアが2027年2月再販

Summary

KADOKAWA will resell "Himejima Akeno 15th Anniversary ver." and "Rias Gremory 15th Anniversary ver." from "High School DxD" in February 2027 with renewed packaging. The price is 36,850 yen each. We started accepting reservations from July 23rd.

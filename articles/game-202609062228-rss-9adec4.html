Ahahastudio unveiled the Steam store page for its PC game “FEEDSTOCK” today (September 6, 2026). This cooperative horror game lets you gather resources by feeding your partner items collected from houses, but as your partner grows larger, it becomes increasingly difficult to escape. The game supports up to four players and features in-game voice chat.

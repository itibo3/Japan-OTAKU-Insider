Details (EN)

A collaboration between the TV anime “Detective Conan” based on the original work by Gosho Aoyama and glasses brand Zoff. The eyewear collection ``Zoff | Detective Conan,'' which features the motifs of Conan Edogawa, Ran Mouri, Ai Haibara, Heiji Hattori, Tooru Amuro, and Shuichi Akai, is available for pre-order until June 30th at Zoff's official online store and other locations.

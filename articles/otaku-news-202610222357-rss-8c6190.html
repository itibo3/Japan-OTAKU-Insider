Details (EN)

The feature-length animated film *I Can Hear the Song of the Stars*, produced entirely using generative AI, was released for free on YouTube on October 22, 2026—"Anime Day." Created by a three-person production team over three months of active work—involving approximately 1,500 shots and more than 10,000 rounds of trial and error—the film depicts the journey of an executive who has come to measure himself solely by his achievements as he regains his sense of self.

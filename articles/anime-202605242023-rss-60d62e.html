Details (EN)

A revival screening of the anime ``Made in Abyss the Movie [Part 1] Dawn of Departure'' based on the original work by Akihito Tsukushi will be held from May 22nd to June 4th. Today, May 24th, a stage greeting commemorating the revival screening was held at Shinjuku Wald 9 in Tokyo, where Miyu Tomita who played Riko, Mariya Ise who played Reg, and Shiori Izawa who played Nanachi took the stage.

Japanese title

「HG 1/144 ガンダムグリープ」がガンダムベース東京に展示中。新規デザイン/造型でリニューアルされた可変MS【ガンダムベース撮り下ろし】

Summary

The variable MS "Gundam Greep" that appears in "New Mobile Report Gundam W DUAL STORY G-UNIT" has been made into a plastic model with a new look and released at Premium Bandai. A sample of it is on display at Gundam Base Tokyo.

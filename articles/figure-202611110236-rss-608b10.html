Details (EN)

7-Eleven Japan will hold a collaboration event with Bandai’s “Tamagotchi” mobile game starting November 13. Ahead of the event, the “7-Eleven Tamagotchi Fair PR Press Conference” was held at 7-Eleven Japan’s headquarters in Chiyoda Ward, Tokyo. At the event, it was announced that a variety of collaborative products would showcase the characters and world of “Tamagotchi,” which celebrates its 30th anniversary on November 23.

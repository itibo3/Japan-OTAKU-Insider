Details (EN)

FutureFriendsGames today released the PC game "SunsetSummit" developed by Joe and Andrew Fender. This is a mountain climbing action game where you control grandpa and grandma and aim to reach the top before sunset. It supports multiplayer for up to 8 people, and you can try 16 different mountain climbing courses across 4 floating islands.

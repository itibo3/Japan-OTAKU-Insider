Details (EN)

On May 20, 2026, Embracer Group announced its intention to split the group into two companies. The company aims to separate Fellowship Entertainment, which focuses on IP such as "Lord of the Rings" and "Tomb Raider," and list it on Nasdaq Stockholm in 2027. The remaining Embracer will develop its game and entertainment business, including THQNordic and PLAION.

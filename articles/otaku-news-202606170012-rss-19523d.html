This summer, Universal Studios Japan (USJ) will deliver a ``festive'' summer where both adults and children can push their limits and play to the fullest all day long, including the new night entertainment ``Universal Summer Matsuri Night ~Neon Glow-up~'' starting July 1, 2026. Along with this, cool sweets and drinks from park favorites such as My Melody & Kuromi and Minions, as well as cool goods to beat the heat, will also be available.

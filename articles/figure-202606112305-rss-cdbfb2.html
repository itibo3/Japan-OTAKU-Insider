Japanese title

ピットロード、グレートウォールホビー製品の取り扱いを6月11日をもって終了

Summary

Pit Road announced that as of June 11, 2026, it will no longer be handling Great Wall Hobby (G.W.H Model, plastic model kits made by Shanghai Lion Roar Model Technology Co., Ltd.) products that the company sold as a Japanese import agent.

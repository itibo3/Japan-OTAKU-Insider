Details (EN)

It has been announced that the TV anime *The Person I’ve Been Thinking About Isn’t a Guy* will begin airing on January 8, 2027, in the “Furaani” time slot on the Nippon TV network. To mark this announcement, the first promotional video—featuring footage from the anime and its original soundtrack—has been released. It has also been revealed that Kenjiro Tsuda, Yu Hatanaka, Yurina Amami, Ai Fairouz, and Maiko Nomura will join the cast as the voices of additional characters.

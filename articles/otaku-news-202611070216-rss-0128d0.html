Details (EN)

“Armored Trooper Votoms: The Gray Witch (Hekse),” a brand-new installment in the “Armored Trooper Votoms” series celebrating Sunrise’s 50th anniversary, will be released nationwide on November 20, 2026. With the release just two weeks away, new stills from the film have been released. In addition, details regarding theater merchandise for the film have been announced, and a “Worldview Explanation Video” by Sumire Uesaka—a self-proclaimed “Bottomz-loving voice actress” who also appears in the film—has been released.

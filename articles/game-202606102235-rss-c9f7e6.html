Solo developer Ichiji has announced a new title currently under development, ``Travel in the Rain.'' This work is a story-oriented 2D adventure game set on an isolated island covered in rain, where players search for their way home while visiting various eras and places. Through exploration, puzzles, branching conversations, and more, you will get closer to the truth about the island.

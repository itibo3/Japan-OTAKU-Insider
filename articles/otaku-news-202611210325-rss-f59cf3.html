Composer Yuki Hayashi, a former rhythmic gymnast, mastered film scoring after meeting Hiroyuki Sawano and has continued to evolve his work on long-running series such as *My Hero Academia* and *Haikyū!!*. Through *Gakuhou Shokudo IV*, he has broken new ground in the fusion of visuals and music and has also expanded his activities to include overseas performances.

The premiere date for the TV anime *The Person I Like Likes* has been set for April 2027, and a teaser visual, teaser PV, and details on the main cast and staff have been revealed. The cast includes Taketo Kajiwara as Kanaya Akizuki, Kazuki Mikawa as Asahi Suzukaze, and Nene Hieda as Miya Fuwa. The series will be directed by Kazuo Koga, with animation production handled by project No.9.

Volume 5 [AA] of Eiji Masuda’s manga series *I’m Being Rocked Again This Morning* went on sale on the 17th. The promotional tagline on the obi reads, “The Hottest Thing Right Now! A Train-Set Cheer-Up Romantic Comedy ♥,” while the back cover states, “The Passengers’ Days of Agonizing Anticipation! A New-Style Cheer-Up Romantic Comedy!” In his release announcement, Eiji Masuda wrote, “The aftermath of the fireworks festival… Finally, to my friend...”

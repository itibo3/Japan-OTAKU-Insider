Japanese title

埴輪と土偶がカプセル可動フィギュアに？ 「UGOMO 埴輪と土偶」がガシャポンオンラインにて7月15日に予約開始

Summary

Bandai will begin accepting pre-orders for the Gashapon ``UGOMO Haniwa and Dogu'' at the online store ``Gashapon Online'' from 11:00 on July 15th. Shipping is scheduled for late October, and the price is 500 yen per shipment.

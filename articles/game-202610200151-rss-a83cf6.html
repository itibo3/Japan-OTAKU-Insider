On October 18, 2026, BASEDKICK announced that it will release its new title currently in development, “HYPNOSYNC,” on October 28 at 7:00 p.m. Japan Standard Time. In this rhythm-and-hypnosis-themed music game, players take on the role of the manager of a mysterious arcade and, under their boss’s orders, hypnotize visitors to turn them into “employees.”

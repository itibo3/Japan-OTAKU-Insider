Details (EN)

Aniplex has announced “LunaReverse,” a new title developed in collaboration with Cybird for the first time, for Nintendo Switch and Steam, with plans for a simultaneous global release. This adventure game depicts the fate that awaits at the end of a quest for revenge. A teaser visual and promotional video have been released, with Tomomi Nakayama and Ichiha handling the scenario and Ugonba serving as character designer.

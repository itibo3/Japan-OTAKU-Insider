When you hear “the three great RPGs,” what comes to mind? Setting aside the third spot for now, when it comes to Japanese titles, the top two—*Dragon Quest* and *Final Fantasy*—are likely non-negotiable for many people. The book I’m introducing today, *Chronicle of Japanese RPGs*, is a critical work that focuses on these two giants and seeks to trace the “vertical axis” of RPGs from their dawn to the present day.

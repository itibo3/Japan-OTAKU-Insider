Details (EN)

Medicos Entertainment will release the “Kana 1/4 Reverse Bunny Ver.” figure in August 2027 for 25,850 yen. The tights on the standard edition are made of fabric to reproduce the texture and feel of the original, and the Medicus Distribution Limited Edition “Kana 1/4 Bare Legs Reverse Bunny Ver.” will also be released that same month.

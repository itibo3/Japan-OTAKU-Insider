The second edition of "GUNDAM STAMP RALLY 2026" will be held from June 8th to July 12th, 2026 in the Tokyo Waterfront City area centered around Daiba and Aomi. This is the final activity of the "TOKYO Gundam Project" as the installation of the life-sized Unicorn Gundam statue ends at the end of August, and a stamp designed with the motif of the statue in awakening mode against the night sky will be released.

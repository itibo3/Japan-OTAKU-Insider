"Hatsune Miku" based on the "Seiryu" motif drawn by Suyan Lily is now available as a 1/7 scale figure! Scheduled to be released in September 2027

Published: 2026-08-17
ID: figure-202608171944-rss-ee3c9f
Category: figure
Source: https://hobby.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2133020.html

Japanese title

苏安Lily氏が描きおろした「青龍」をモチーフにアレンジされた「初音ミク」が1/7スケールフィギュアで登場！2027年9月発売予定

Summary

Kaiyodo will release the figure "Hatsune Miku Seiryu Ver. 1/7 Scale Figure" in September 2027. Reservations are scheduled to start from 12:00 on August 17th, and the reservation period is until 3:00 pm on October 16th. The price is 27,500 yen.

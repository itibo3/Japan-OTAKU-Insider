Details (EN)

On October 29, 2026, 23First announced that “The Orphaned House,” a first-person adventure game currently in development by CharmGames, had received certification from the private certification service “ProudlyHuman” (Human-Made). This marks the first instance of such certification in the gaming sector for this service, and the certification mark may now be displayed on the game’s images and promotional materials.

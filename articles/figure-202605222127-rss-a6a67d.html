Toys R Us Japan will hold the grand opening of the Toys R Us Kamade Clock store on May 22nd in Kameido Clock, a large commercial facility in Kameido, Tokyo. Furthermore, on June 5th, the Toys R Us Chikushino store will be relocated to the grand opening of Aeon Mall Chikushino, a large commercial facility in Chikushino, Fukuoka Prefecture.

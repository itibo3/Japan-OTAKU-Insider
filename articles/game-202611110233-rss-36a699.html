Details (EN)

LoertingGames today released the PC game "WhattheBuck?!," developed by kohlgames, on Steam. This game is an incremental game where players destroy money. Players swipe their credit card through an ATM and destroy the bills that fall onto the table. They earn money based on how much they destroy, which they can then use to purchase upgrades.

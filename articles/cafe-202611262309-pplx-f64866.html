Details (EN)

A collaboration café themed around the retro-pop style of *Girls & Panzer: More Love-Love Operation!* will open sequentially starting December 11 at three *Shirare CAFE* locations in Tokyo, Nagoya, and Osaka. The café will offer merchandise featuring newly drawn illustrations of Miho Nishizumi and the other five characters, as well as food and drinks inspired by the characters.

It is recommended to watch the "Venom" series in release order (2018 → 2021 → 2024), and with U-NEXT you can watch the series all at once. The series is completed in three parts, with a total running time of approximately 5 hours and 19 minutes. ``Spider-Man: No Way Home'' is a related work that connects the story from the mid-credits scene of Venom 2, and if you watch it together, you can get a deeper taste of the foreshadowing of the multiverse.

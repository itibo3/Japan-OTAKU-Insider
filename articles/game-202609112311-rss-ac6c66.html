Details (EN)

Beyond the door opened by *Black Myth: Wukong* lies an AAA action RPG where you can “experience” Chinese creation myths. Developed by China’s Genigods Lab, *Genigods: Nezha* is a single-player action RPG based on Chinese creation myths featuring figures such as Nüwa, Pangu, Houyi, and Nezha. We interviewed Young Lau, CEO and producer at Genigods Lab.

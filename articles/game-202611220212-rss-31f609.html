Details (EN)

KRAFTON is showcasing the PC version of “AgeTwisters” at “G-STAR 2026,” a gaming event currently being held in South Korea. This game is a co-op-only adventure title in which two players each control a grandfather and a granddaughter. There are four age levels—ranging from child to elderly—and both players can switch between them freely.

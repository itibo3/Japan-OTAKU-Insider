Voice actors Nozomi Urawa and Nobuhiko Okamoto, who star in the anime *Togen Ankī*, will make guest appearances on “VS. Super Express,” which airs every Monday at 12:59 a.m. on the Nippon TV network. Together with Super Express, they will take on challenges such as the “What-If Scenario: Line-Recitation Game” and “Quiz! Read-Read Masterpiece Don!”

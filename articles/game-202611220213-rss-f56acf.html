Details (EN)

PALM is showcasing the PC game “KISI: The DejaVu” at “G-STAR 2026,” a gaming event currently being held in South Korea. This title is a horror game centered on the theme of déjà vu. Players take on the role of a test subject in a psychological experiment conducted by a mad scientist, moving back and forth between two mirror-image rooms to search for “anomalies” in a “spot the difference” style.

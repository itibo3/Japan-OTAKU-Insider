Volume 1 [AA] of Riku Natsume’s manga *Debt Magical Girl Kurekariboka* is already on sale in Akihabara. It’s a story about a girl who makes a contract with a mysterious creature named Kurekure and fights monsters as a magical girl. The promotional tagline on the obi reads, “A magical girl can’t fight without revolving credit!?” while the back cover states, “Revolving credit—it’s the gateway to hell right under your nose...”

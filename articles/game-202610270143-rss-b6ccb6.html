To commemorate the 30th anniversary of “Pokémon,” all 22 varieties of the limited-edition “Pilkul 400” packaging will go on sale sequentially starting November 2—featuring the 51 Pokémon from the Kanto region and Beedrill, along with a campaign offering a chance to win special merchandise.

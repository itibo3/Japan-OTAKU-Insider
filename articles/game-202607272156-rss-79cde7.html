BadTapeStudios has released a trailer for a new title currently under development, ``Kaho Hotline Midnight Manager.'' This work is a horror simulation set in the Kaho Building, an apartment complex filled with secrets, in which the player plays the role of a night-shift manager who responds to calls from residents. While listening to the calls and checking the instructions, you will find answers to the strange consultations.

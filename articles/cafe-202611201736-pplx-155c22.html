Details (EN)

A collaboration café for “Shishiwata Cooking,” featuring Hololive’s Botan Shishiro and Watae Kakumaki, will be held at three SMILE BASE CAFE locations. Starting with the Nagoya Sakae location on November 26, the event will tour the three locations, where visitors can enjoy a collaboration menu curated by the two streamers and participate in “Shishiwata 3-Minute Cooking” sessions accompanied by newly recorded voice messages.

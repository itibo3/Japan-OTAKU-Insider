Details (EN)

The "Life-Sized Unicorn Gundam Statue" installed at DiverCity Tokyo Plaza in Odaiba will end its exhibition at the end of August 2026. The landmark, which has been loved by many fans and tourists for 8 years and 11 months since its release in September 2017, will be removed, and special exhibitions and events are being planned for the finale.

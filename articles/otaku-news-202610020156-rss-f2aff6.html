Details (EN)

Universal Studios Japan (USJ) will host “Universal Cool Japan 2027” starting December 2, 2026. As the second installment of its lineup announcements, a collaboration with the global hit series *The Apothecary’s Tale*—which is also generating excitement for its third season of the TV anime set to air starting October 2—has been announced. The collaboration will run for a limited time starting February 10, 2027, and will feature new programs tied to the upcoming film, *The Apothecary’s Tale: The Secret Treasure of the Late Empress*.

Details (EN)

It has been announced that the ``Uma Musume's Uma Uma! Kentucky!'' project, which is a collaboration with the game ``Uma Musume Pretty Derby'' (hereinafter referred to as ``Uma Musume''), will be held at Kentucky Fried Chicken (hereinafter referred to as ``KFC'') stores nationwide from August 12th to September 30th, 2026. During the period, original collaboration products developed with the work in mind will be sold, original collaboration songs and wrapping shops will also appear, and there will be a lottery to win luxurious prizes.

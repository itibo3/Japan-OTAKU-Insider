Marvelous announced today that, as part of a restructuring of its consumer business, it will implement an external career transition support program with approximately 65 openings. The application period runs from December 14, 2026, to January 8, 2027. The company is reviewing its business and development structures to adapt to the increasing scale and duration of game development projects.

Details (EN)

The final venue for the major exhibition “Gege Akutami’s *Jujutsu Kaisen*: The Complete Exhibition,” which unravels the “behind-the-scenes stories” of Gege Akutami’s manga *Jujutsu Kaisen*, will open on December 25, 2026, at the Shinjuku Sumitomo Building Triangle Plaza in Tokyo. Registration for the advance lottery ticket sales has now begun. Additionally, details have been released regarding an additional exhibit that thoroughly explains the creative process behind the spin-off series *Jujutsu Kaisen ≡ (Modulo)*, as well as information on original merchandise—including a *Special Edition* of the official exhibition catalog—that will be available for the first time at the Shinjuku venue.

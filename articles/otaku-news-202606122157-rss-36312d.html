As part of the 10th anniversary project for the theatrical animation series ``KING OF PRISM,'' it has been decided that an eight-and-a-half-hour broadcast will be broadcast on NTV Plus on June 21st. Seven popular works will be broadcast, including the latest work ``Kintsa'', which will be broadcast for the first time on TV. The route that ranks first based on viewer voting will be broadcast.

Details (EN)

"Gintama: The New Movie - The Great Yoshiwara Fire - <Super Blaze Edition>" is currently being re-released in theaters nationwide starting September 25, 2026. On September 26, the second day of its release, a commemorative stage greeting was held at Shinjuku Wald 9 in Tokyo. The first stage greeting featured Tomokazu Sugita (voicing Gintoki), Daisuke Sakaguchi (voicing Shinpachi), and Rie Kugimiya (voicing Kagura).

Details (EN)

The science adventure series *STEINS;GATE* is collaborating with Tokyo public bathhouses. Events will be held at 15 locations, including Sugamo-yu, starting Friday, November 27, 2026, and running through Sunday, December 27, 2026. The facilities will feature sales of original merchandise, special bath perks, a stamp rally, a unique bath experience called “Future Gadget Prototype Gelbana Bath,” and in-store decorations.

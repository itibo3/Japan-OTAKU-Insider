A concept room stay plan where you can experience the world of the TV anime "Ranma 1/2" will be available at Sunshine City Prince Hotel in Ikebukuro, Tokyo. With the theme of "Summer of the Showa Era," a Japanese-style room inspired by Tendo-ya, the setting in the story, will be developed, and will be on sale from July 25, 2026 to October 24, 2026.

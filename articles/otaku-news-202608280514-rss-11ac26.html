To commemorate the broadcast of the latest work of the Pierrot Magical Girl Series, Magic Sisters Lulut Lily, produced by Studio Pierrot, the Pierrot Magical Girl Series Anniversary Blu-ray BOX will be released every other month from April 2026. The walnut box design drawn by Yoshiyuki Kishi and the contents of the new audio commentary have been released for the fourth installment of the series, ``Magical Idol Pastel Yuumi'', which will be released on October 28th.

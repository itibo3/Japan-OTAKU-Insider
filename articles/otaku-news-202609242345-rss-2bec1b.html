Details (EN)

It has been announced that the manga *Oishinbo* will be adapted into a TV anime titled *New Oishinbo*—35 years after the previous anime series ended—and will air in 2027. Yohei Azagami will voice Shiro Yamaoka, Hitomi Sakuragi will voice Yuko Kurita, and Akio Otsuka will voice Yuzan Umihara. Produced by Shin-Ei Animation, the series will make its long-awaited return as a direct sequel. A teaser visual was released alongside the announcement, and comments from the main cast have also been shared.

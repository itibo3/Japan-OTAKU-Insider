SME announced today that it has entered into a share transfer agreement to acquire shares in GungHo, as well as a capital and business alliance agreement with the company. The two companies plan to pursue a wide range of collaborations by combining GungHo’s leading IPs—such as “Puzzle & Dragons”—along with its game development capabilities and expertise, with the Sony Music Group’s knowledge and experience in the entertainment sector.

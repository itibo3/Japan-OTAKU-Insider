Details (EN)

It has been revealed that LiSA, an artist known for performing numerous anime theme songs and insert songs—including those for *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba*—has divorced Tatsuhisa Suzuki, a voice actor known for roles such as Makoto Tachibana in *Free!*. The news was reported on LiSA’s official website on November 28, 2026, and Suzuki also announced it on his official fan club website.

Details (EN)

The live tour "IDOLiSH7 VISIBLIVE TOUR '4WARD JOURNEY'" by 16 idols of "IDOLiSH7" began on August 21, 2026 at Makuhari Messe International Exhibition Center 1-3. This tour will bring together four groups: IDOLiSH7, TRIGGER, Re:vale, and ŹOOĻ, starting in Makuhari and continuing to INTEX Osaka Building 5 from August 28th to 30th, and Marine Messe Fukuoka Building B on September 13th. In this article, we look back at the excitement surrounding the first performance.

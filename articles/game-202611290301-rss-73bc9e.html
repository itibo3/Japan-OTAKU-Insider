Details (EN)

The monthly series “My Collaboration Café,” published on the fourth Saturday of every month, is a column in which Tetsuya Onishi—a working chef—creates his own collaborations with various games and other sources to introduce recipes inspired by them. This installment’s theme is *Romancing SaGa 3*, with the full remake *Romancing SaGa 3: Destiny United* set for release in February 2027. We asked him to interpret the game’s greatsword technique, “Chaotic Snow, Moon, and Flower,” using three-colored curries representing snow, moon, and flower.

Details (EN)

Volume 1 [AA] of the manga series “Vampire Princess Miyui,” based on the original work by Toshihiro Hirano and illustrated by Narumi Kakinouchi, officially went on sale on the 12th. The promotional tagline on the dust jacket reads, “〈Miyu〉 and 〈Yui〉—The fates of the two vampire princesses finally intersect!”, while the manga description states, “A new story is now unfolding, woven from the encounter and intertwining of these two vampire princesses...”

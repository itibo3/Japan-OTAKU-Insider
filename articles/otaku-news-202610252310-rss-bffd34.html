A special package has been launched in collaboration with the anime series *Nintama Rantarō*, allowing visitors to enjoy collaborative events at Atami Onsen and Noboribetsu Onsen. In addition to a digital stamp rally in the hot spring towns, collaborative food offerings, and character-themed decorations, accommodation packages that include limited-edition merchandise will also be available for purchase.

Details (EN)

From the TV anime *"There's No Way I Could Be a Girlfriend, No Way, No Way! (*Or Was It Possible!?)"*, swimsuit figures of Rena Amai, Mayu Otsuka, and Ayaka Sena are now available as part of the "POP UP PARADE BEACH QUEENS" series. Priced at 8,800 yen each (tax included), pre-orders are now being accepted on various e-commerce sites from 12:00 PM on Friday, November 20, 2026, through 11:59 PM on Wednesday, December 23, 2026. Shipping and general sales are scheduled to begin sequentially starting in June 2027.

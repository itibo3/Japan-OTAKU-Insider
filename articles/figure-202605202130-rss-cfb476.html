Details (EN)

Aoshima Bunka Kyozaisha will begin accepting reservations for the plastic model ``RakuPla Snap Car No.09 EK9 Civic Type R 1997'' in three colors from 2:00 p.m. on May 27th. Release is scheduled for October, and the price is 4,180 yen each. There are three colors: Championship White, Starlight Black Pearl, and Borg Silver Metallic.

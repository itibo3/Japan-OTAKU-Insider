Details (EN)

In collaboration with the manga ``Kingdom'', Saga Prefecture's ``Kingdom x (Running) Saga Prefecture ~ Let's not let the fire of Saga die out'' project will start a new project, ``Furuyu Kingdom Onsenkyo,'' from July 17, 2026. Eight photo spots will appear in the hot spring town, including a giant Ouki panel measuring approximately 9m wide x 8m high, and a stamp rally will be held where more and more members of the "Hishintai" will join in each step.

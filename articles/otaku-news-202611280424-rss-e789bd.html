It has been announced that the manga *Shark: Devouring the Other World* will be adapted into a TV anime. Under the direction of Shige Fukase, Studio Rings will handle the animation production. Along with the announcement, a teaser trailer and promotional visuals were released, and comments were received from the original author, Kubo Kenya, the main staff, and those involved in the shark-themed visuals.

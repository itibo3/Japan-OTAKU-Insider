It has been revealed that the third season of the TV anime ``Ranma 1/2'' will be broadcast nationwide on Nippon Television from October 3, 2026, and will be exclusively distributed on Netflix immediately after the broadcast. Along with this, the key visual for the third season has been released. It has also been revealed that Masako Nozawa, Jun Fukuyama, Mayumi Tanaka, Saori Hayami, and Mimi Tanaka will be cast as additional characters, and comments have arrived.

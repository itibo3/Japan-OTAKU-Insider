Details (EN)

Koei Tecmo Games announced today (October 9, 2026) that it has begun accepting applications for the official 4th-anniversary meetup for *Nobunaga’s Ambition: Hado*. The event is scheduled to take place on December 6 at the KT Building in Yokohama. The event will feature opportunities to interact with the executive producer and cast members of the official livestream, as well as a quiz contest. The event is limited to 40 participants, and the application deadline is October 18 at 11:59 PM.

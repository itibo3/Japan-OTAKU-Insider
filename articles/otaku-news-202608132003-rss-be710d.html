Details (EN)

Chiikawa's first smartphone app, Chiikawa Pocket, is opening the Chiipoke Observation Deck on the summit of Mt. Bizan, the symbol of Tokushima City, in conjunction with Awa Odori, one of Japan's three major Bon dances, which is being held in Tokushima City, Tokushima Prefecture from August 11th to 15th, 2026. This event is being held as part of the "Chiipoke Summer Festival 2026," and on August 12th, Amico Building started distributing "Chiipoke Summer Fans," which were crowded with many people who came to the festival.

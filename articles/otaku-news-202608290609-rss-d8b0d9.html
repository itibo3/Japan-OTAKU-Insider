Details (EN)

To coincide with the end of the exhibition of the “Life-Size Unicorn Gundam Statue,” commemorative merchandise from “STRICT-G”—an apparel shop inspired by the Gundam universe—is now available. Starting August 29, 2026, these items will be available exclusively at the STRICT-G Odaiba store. Ahead of this, pre-orders are currently being accepted on “Premium Bandai,” the official online store of the Bandai Namco Group.

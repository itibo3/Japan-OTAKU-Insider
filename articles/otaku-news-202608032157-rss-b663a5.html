Details (EN)

“ONE PIECE DAY’26” will be held on August 22nd and 23rd, 2026 at Exhibition Halls 4 to 6 of the Makuhari Messe International Exhibition Center in Chiba. It has now been revealed that the stage will be streamed live for free on X (formerly Twitter) and LINE this year in addition to YouTube. In addition, the project ``WORLD TOP 100 Prediction King'' has begun to predict the final ranking of the character world popularity poll, and the stage timetable and gorgeous performers have also been released.

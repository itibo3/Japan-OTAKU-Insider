Details (EN)

ClearRiverGames announced today that it will release the action RPG "Creation of the World" on January 14, 2027. This is a revival of the original 1995 version for current-generation consoles, featuring new additions such as "Arrange Mode," a rewind function, and a music player. A playable demo is also being showcased at the Tokyo Game Show 2026.

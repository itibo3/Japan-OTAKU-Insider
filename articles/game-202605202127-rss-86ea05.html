Details (EN)

Gerappa today announced that the PC game "Sangokushi BOND" will be released in the summer of 2026. This work is a deck-building roguelite military battle based on Romance of the Three Kingdoms. Recruit randomly presented military commanders, read your opponent's strategy, and build your formation. It supports one-on-one online battles and has two modes: synchronous battle and asynchronous battle.

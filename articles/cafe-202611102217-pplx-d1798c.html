Details (EN)

The collaboration menu available at “at Share CAFE,” the collaboration café for the TV anime *Moriarty the Patriot*, and the lineup of original merchandise featuring watercolor-style designs have been unveiled. The event will run in Tokyo from November 19 to November 29, 2026; in Nagoya from November 21 to November 30; and in Osaka’s Shinsaibashi as an additional event from December 3 to December 7. Reservations will be accepted on a first-come, first-served basis starting at 7:00 PM on November 11.

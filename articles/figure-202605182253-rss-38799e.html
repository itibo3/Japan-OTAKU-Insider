Details (EN)

Sony Music Entertainment, Bandai Namco Entertainment, and BANDAI SPIRITS will be offering ``High Quality Gunpla Scan,'' which scans user-customized Gunpla and provides 3D model data from May 30th to September 27th. The venue is THE GUNDAM BASE TOKYO. Reservations are being accepted starting May 18th from 3:00 pm, and the participation fee is 2,200 yen.

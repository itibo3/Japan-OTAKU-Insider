Details (EN)

The collaboration project of the TV anime "Funeral Free Ren", "A journey of stars and bonfires that creates memories", will be held for a limited time from July 17th to October 16th, 2026 at the "Farm Resort THE FARM" (hereinafter referred to as "THE FARM") in Katori City, Chiba Prefecture. It has recently been revealed that in this project, original sweets that reproduce the dessert "Merku Pudding" that appears in the work will be provided.

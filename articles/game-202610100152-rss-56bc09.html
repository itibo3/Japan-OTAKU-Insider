ClumsyBearStudio announced today (October 9, 2026) that it will release the official Version 1.0 of “Hungry Horrors,” currently in Early Access on Steam, on October 13. The price is 1,700 yen (tax included). This deck-building adventure game challenges players to serve meals to monsters and satisfy them before the players themselves get eaten.

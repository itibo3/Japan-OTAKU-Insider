Episode 42 of “Tsuzukimi,” a special program featuring a marathon viewing of new promotional videos for Winter 2027 anime, is set to stream starting at 5:30 p.m. on December 23, 2026. It will be broadcast live on Niconico Live and Bandai Channel, among other platforms, and applications are being accepted for on-site viewing at the Animate Theater in Ikebukuro.

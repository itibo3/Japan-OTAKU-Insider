Details (EN)

On November 5, 2026, the City of Yokohama launched a special light show on the “Cosmo Clock 21” Ferris wheel as part of the “Star Rail Pioneering Game in Yokohama,” a collaboration campaign with the space fantasy RPG “Honkai: Star Rail.” During the event period, the Star God ★ Ahha will appear on the Ferris wheel at night. Starting November 11, a special exhibition featuring character panels and other displays is scheduled to begin at Hammerhead Park.

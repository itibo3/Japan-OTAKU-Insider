Details (EN)

Pokémon announced today (September 24, 2026) that it will add the “High Class Pack MEGA” expansion pack to the smartphone card game “Pokémon Trading Card Game Pocket” for a limited time starting at 10:00 a.m. on September 30. This special expansion pack features a large selection of cards that originally appeared in sets ranging from “Mega Rising” to “Ruler of the Skies.”

Details (EN)

The ``1st Movie Theater Award'' award ceremony, sponsored by the ``Let's Go to the Movie Theater!'' Executive Committee, was held today, May 12th, at 109 Cinemas Premium Shinjuku in Tokyo. At the award ceremony, Hiro Shimono, who plays Zenitsu Agatsuma from ``Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba the Movie: Mugenjou Chapter 1: Resurrection of Inugosza,'' Saori Hayami, who plays Shinobu Kocho, a Conan costume from ``Detective Conan: Fallen Angel on the Highway,'' and producer Yuhei Okada of TMS Entertainment appeared on stage. The ambassadors were LiLiCo, a movie commentator, and LEO (BE:FIRST), who is also known as a movie lover.

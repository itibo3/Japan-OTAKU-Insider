Details (EN)

It has been announced that the original TV anime ``Moebius Dust'' produced by the animation studio ``Project ANIMA'' will be broadcast from July 2026. The main cast will be Yuto Takenaka, Nene Hieda, and Haruka Sato. In addition, the opening theme has been decided to be Leo Ieiri's "Mobius", and the opening video of the first episode has also been released.

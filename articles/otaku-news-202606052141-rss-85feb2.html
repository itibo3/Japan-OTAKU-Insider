Details (EN)

Each Ghibli film is an independent, one-story story, so you can basically enjoy them in any order. If you are watching the series for the first time, we recommend that you start with the classic five works: ``My Neighbor Totoro,'' ``Kiki's Delivery Service,'' ``Castle in the Sky,'' ``Spirited Away,'' and ``Princess Mononoke.'' We will introduce a total of 25 works in order of release year, from 1984's ``Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind'' to 2023's ``How Do You Live?''

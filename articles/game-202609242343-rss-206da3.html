Details (EN)

EXNOA announced today that it will terminate service for the simulation RPG "Rejeclo! ~Legend Clover~" at 12:00 p.m. on October 29, 2026. The game launched in October 2021 and will bring its approximately five-year run to a close. Until the service ends, various events are planned to express gratitude for the past five years.

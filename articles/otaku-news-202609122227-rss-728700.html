Details (EN)

The two-hour special of the TV anime *Detective Conan*, titled *Detective Conan: The Murder on the 30th*, will air on September 25 as part of the “Friday Roadshow” program. The main visual and promotional video have now been released, and Mai Kuraki has been confirmed as the artist for the theme song. It has also been announced that an episode titled “The Crimson Final Performance” will be adapted from the regular series.

The Korea Internet PC Cafe Cooperative Association (KIPC) has applied for a provisional disposition against RiotGamesKorea at the Seoul Central District Court. The conflict between the two parties has been brought to court over an increase in premium service rates for PC shops and a notice to cut off game connections to stores that have stopped paying fees.

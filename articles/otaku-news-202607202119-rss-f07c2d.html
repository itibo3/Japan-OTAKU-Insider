Details (EN)

Original full-length animated film “Super Kaguyahime!” It has been announced that the collaboration project ``Sunshine Aquarium x Super Kaguyahime! Tsukuyomi Aquarium'' will be held only at night at Sunshine Aquarium in Ikebukuro, Tokyo from July 24th to August 30th, 2026. The virtual space ``Tsukuyomi'' from the work will appear in the aquarium on a summer night, and you can enjoy a special production of jellyfish, lights, and music as well as a mermaid show, as well as collaboration commemorative goods featuring newly drawn illustrations.

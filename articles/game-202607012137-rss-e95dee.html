Crowdfunding is underway for an unofficial cookbook inspired by ``The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom'' and ``The Legend of Zelda: Wisdom Ornaments.'' The author has written many books that recreate cooking from games, manga, and anime, and in addition to containing dozens of recipes with photos, the set also includes a booklet summarizing cooking terms.

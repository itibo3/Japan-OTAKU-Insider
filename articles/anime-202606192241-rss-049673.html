"Super Kaguya Hime!" will be revived in special formats such as 4D from September 18th, special announcement released

Published: 2026-06-19
ID: anime-202606192241-rss-049673
Category: anime
Source: https://natalie.mu/comic/news/676938

Japanese title

「超かぐや姫！」9月18日より4Dなど特別フォーマットで復活上映、特報を公開

Summary

A revival screening of the original animated film "Super Kaguyahime!" has been decided. From September 18th, in addition to the regular version, it will be re-screened in MX4D, 4DX, SCREENX, ULTRA 4DX, and Dolby Atmos formats.

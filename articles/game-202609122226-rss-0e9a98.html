Details (EN)

WHISKYMEW announced that it will begin accepting applications for a lottery-based sale of two types of labeled whiskey based on Cygames’ cross-media content “Uma Musume Pretty Derby” at 12:00 p.m. on September 25, 2026. Inspired by Dream Journey and Epiphaneia, these products will be sold in limited quantities, each featuring a label depicting the respective Uma Musume character.

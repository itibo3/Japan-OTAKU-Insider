Details (EN)

The screening event ``Kaoru Shintani's 75th Birthday Commemoration Theatrical Version & OVA Area 88 ~Flaming Unicorn Screening~'' was held on April 26th at Ikebukuro's Shinbungeiza, hosted by Robo Ishimaru, who is involved in planning numerous screening events as an eventer. At the event, the movie version of ``Area 88'' and the OVA version of ``Area 88 ACT III Burning Mirage'' were screened, as well as a talk stage where Kaoru Shintani, the original author of ``Area 88'', appeared as a guest.

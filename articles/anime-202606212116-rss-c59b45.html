Details (EN)

An advance screening of the TV anime series ``Futsutsukana Akujo de Wajisuga - Hinamiya Chonu Torikaeden'' with stage greetings from the cast was held today, June 21st, at TOHO Cinemas Hibiya in Tokyo. At the stage greeting held after the screening, Natsumi Kawaida who played the role of Shu Huizue, Kana Hishikawa who played the role of Riri, Nikelai Faranadze who played the role of Huang Dongxue, and special supporters Madoka Seikaze and Junka who played the former Takarazuka top girls appeared on stage.

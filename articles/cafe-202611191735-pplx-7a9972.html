"Share CAFE," operated by Anique, will host a collaboration café with *Girls und Panzer: It's a More Love-Love Operation!* at its three locations nationwide. Merchandise featuring newly drawn illustrations based on the theme and special collaboration menu items will be available, and reservations will be accepted on a first-come, first-served basis starting at 6:00 PM on November 20. The event at the Harakado branch in Tokyo will run from December 11 to 20.

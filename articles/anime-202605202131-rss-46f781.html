Details (EN)

A collaboration between the TV anime "Flame Fighting Ball Girl Dodge Danko" based on the original work by Tetsuhiro Koshitata and the "35th Summer National Elementary School Dodgeball Championship" has been decided. Manaka Nakayama, who plays the role of Ichigeki Danko, and Kaori Maeda, who plays the role of Chinko Kobutsu, will be on stage at the national tournament to be held on August 16th at the Takasaki Arena in Gunma.

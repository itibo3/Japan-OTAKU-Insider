Details (EN)

It has been announced that Toho Co., Ltd.’s Godzilla Division will launch a capsule toy line called “Godzadama” starting in April 2027. The first series will include the “Godzilla-0.0 Metal Charm,” the “GODZILLA・SANRIO CHARACTERS Mascot Charm” (a collaboration with Sanrio Co., Ltd. characters), and the “Godzilla Locker Key-Style Charm,” which are scheduled to be released sequentially.

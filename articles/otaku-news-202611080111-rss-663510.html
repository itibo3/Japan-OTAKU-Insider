Volume 4 of Sumu Kamiyama’s manga series *Senpai Haimiya Is Scary and Cute*, available in both standard and special editions [AA], went on sale on the 6th. The standard edition’s obi features the tagline “Even if she’s ‘scary,’ I’m hooked,” and the manga description reads: “My heart races whenever I’m around Senpai, but I didn’t know if that meant I ‘liked’ her. Kurose-kun is looking for that ‘something’ to make Senpai notice him...”

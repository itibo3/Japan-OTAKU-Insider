Details (EN)

Kogado Studio today officially announced the city development simulation ``Neosphere: Legacy'', the second remaster of the ``Aoi Series''. This work is a complete renewal of ``Blue Sky Neosphere'' released in 2005, and is set in the aerial kingdom of Neosphere, where the player takes on the challenge of rebuilding the kingdom with Nanoca Franca, a novice inventor at the workshop. Release is scheduled for spring 2027.

It has been announced that *25:00, in Akasaka*, a BL manga by Hiroko Natsuno depicting the romantic relationships between actors, will be adapted into a TV anime. Along with this announcement, a teaser visual has been released, and comments have been shared by the main cast members, Soma Saito and Takuya Sato, as well as the original author, Hiroko Natsuno.

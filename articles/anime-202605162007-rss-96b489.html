The opening day stage greeting for the anime "Mobile Police Patlabor EZY" File 1 was held on May 15th at Shinjuku Piccadilly in Tokyo, with director Yutaka Izubuchi, Kazunori Ito, who worked on the screenplay and series composition, Masami Yuuki, who was in charge of the character design, and Mariko Nagai, who sings the ending theme song "Baton", on stage.

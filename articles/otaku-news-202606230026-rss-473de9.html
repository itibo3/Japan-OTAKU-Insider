Details (EN)

The ``2nd Yamanashi Outdoor Festival Yuru Fes△'' (hereinafter referred to as ``Yuru Fes''), a comprehensive event to promote the appeal of Yamanashi and the outdoors through the anime ``Yuru Camp△'', was held for two days on June 6th and 7th, 2026 at the ``Yochabare Hiroba & Historical Park'' at the north exit of JR Kofu Station in Kofu City, Yamanashi Prefecture. Despite the rain on the second day, the event drew a total of 13,000 people and ended with great success.

Details (EN)

ChocolateDungeo will release the Japanese-compatible PC game "HotelIsHungry" in the second quarter of 2027. This is a psychological horror game in which players run a hotel in a world where humanity has turned into a horrifying state. Players stand at the front desk each morning and choose whether to accept or reject guests. However, only one customer can be turned away per day.

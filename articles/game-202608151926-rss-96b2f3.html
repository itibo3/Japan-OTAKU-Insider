``Ratocalypse,'' currently being developed by CasselGames, is a strategy RPG that automatically fights by setting conditions for using skills on each rat soldier. This is the studio's third mouse game, following ``Ratropolis,'' where you protect a city with cards, and ``Ratopia,'' where you create a city. We spoke to the developer at the "BIC2026" booth.

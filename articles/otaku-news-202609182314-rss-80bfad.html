From *Puella Magi Madoka Magica the Movie: Walpurgis Night’s Turn*, a collaboration with three long-established Kyoto companies featuring designs of Madoka in a maiko outfit and Homura in formal attire is now available. These items will be available for purchase at the “Kyoto International Manga & Anime Fair 2026 (Kyomafu 2026),” taking place on Saturday, September 19, and Sunday, September 20, 2026.

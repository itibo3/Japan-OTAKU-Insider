Details (EN)

Shochiku Games announced today, in conjunction with ``Haiku Day,'' that it will be participating as a co-publisher of the haiku adventure ``OKU'' developed by Irox Games. It is a notable work that has over 300,000 followers on Steam, and allows you to travel through a beautiful world while composing haiku by combining words. It has also been decided to exhibit at Tokyo Game Show 2026.

An exhibition event for *Bobobo-bo Bo-bobo*, titled “Bobobo-bo Bo-bobo Exhibition: DIVE INTO THE BO-BOBO WORLD,” will be held in Ikebukuro, Tokyo, starting September 30, 2026. Featuring exhibits of reproduction original artwork and photo spots, the event will offer a “Hajikerist Training Program” that allows visitors to immerse themselves in the world of the series.

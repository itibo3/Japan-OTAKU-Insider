"INTERSCAPE," developed by South Korea's ROOMTONEGames, is being showcased as a playable title at the G-STAR 2026 game show, which is taking place from November 19 to 22, 2026. It is a story-driven action-adventure game set in a world where people can no longer wake up from their dreams, in which players take on the role of an agent who enters the dreams of others.

Details (EN)

HoYoverse announced today (October 23, 2026) that “Genshin CAFE,” the first official permanent café based on “Genshin Impact,” will open on January 20, 2027, at Yurakucho Marui in Tokyo. The café plans to offer food items inspired by the game’s world and limited-edition merchandise, and a key visual and teaser trailer have also been released.

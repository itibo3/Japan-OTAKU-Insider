Details (EN)

It has been announced that “Kanae Hiyodo,” a new idol to be added to *Gakuen Idolmaster* starting November 16, will be released as a prize figure and a chibi plush. Pre-orders for the Chibi Plush & Pouch Set will begin on Premium Bandai at 11:00 AM on November 16, while the prize figure is scheduled for release around fall 2027.

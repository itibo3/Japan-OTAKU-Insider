Chiya from the manga ``Ane Naru Mono'' is made into a 1/6 scale figure. Reservations start from July 17th exclusively on AmiAmi

ID: figure-202607172026-rss-d6d180

Japanese title

マンガ「姉なるもの」より千夜が1/6スケールフィギュア化。あみあみ限定で7月17日より予約開始

Summary

KADOKAWA began accepting pre-orders for the figure ``Ane Narumono'' Chiya 1/6 Scale Figure on the mail order site ``AmiAmi'' from July 17th. Release is scheduled for February 2027, and the price is 30,800 yen.

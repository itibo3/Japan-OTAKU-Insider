Hajime Ueda's solo exhibition ``USO'' will be held at Déesse space caiman shibuya, a gallery in Shibuya, Tokyo, starting tomorrow, June 13th. The first half will run from June 13th to 28th, and the second half will run from July 3rd to 20th, and there will be changes in exhibits and additions to the exhibits during the exhibition period.

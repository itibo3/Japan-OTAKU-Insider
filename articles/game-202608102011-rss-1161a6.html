Square Enix Holdings today announced its consolidated financial results for the first quarter of the fiscal year ending March 2027. During the fiscal year under review, the digital entertainment business, especially HD games, recorded significant increases in sales and profits. The company explained that in addition to strong sales of new releases such as ``FFVII Rebirth'' and ``The Thousand-Year Tale of Elliot the Adventurer,'' sales of catalog titles also increased.

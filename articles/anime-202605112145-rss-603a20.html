Japanese title

「Dr.STONE」の企画展が宇宙体感施設・Space Travelium TeNQで、アニメ完結に合わせ

Summary

A collaborative exhibition between the TV anime ``Dr.STONE'' based on the original work by Riichiro Inagaki and Boichi and Space Travelium TeNQ, a space experience facility in Tokyo, will be held from July 18th to October 18th.

Yomogi Inumori of “Rabipare!!,” an idol VTuber group whose members fans can meet in person, has released merchandise to celebrate the group’s fourth anniversary. Limited-edition items featuring original artwork by Yomogi herself are also available, and the sale runs until 11:59 p.m. on November 28.

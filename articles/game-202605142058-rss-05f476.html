Details (EN)

Three major companies, Mercari, Rakuma, and Yahoo! Auctions, have announced that they will ban the sale of McDonald's Happy Meal "Chiikawa" toys, which will go on sale from May 15th. McDonald's is also implementing measures to prevent resale, such as requiring purchasing rights in the app, and plans to take strict action against accounts that violate the rules, including restricting their use.

Details (EN)

HoYoverse announced today (October 30, 2026) that it will release Version 4.7, “Now, the Stars Are Enveloped in Laughter,” of the space fantasy RPG *Honkai: Star Rail* on November 11. The Star God ★ Ahha will become a playable character, and the “Two-Phase Paradise” arc will come to a conclusion. A free giveaway will also be held, allowing players to choose one limited ★5 character and one “Mysterious Star Soul” from a selection of three characters, including Yomi.

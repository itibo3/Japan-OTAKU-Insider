Details (EN)

"1/12RC Lunch Box EVO." is a model that inherits the silhouette of the popular RC off-road car "1/12 RC Lunch Box," but with improved running performance. As its name suggests, the ``Lunch Box'' has large tires and a lifted body, making it a rectangular vehicle that looks just like a bento box. The form is inspired by the custom vans that are popular in America, and it has a unique and impressive feel.

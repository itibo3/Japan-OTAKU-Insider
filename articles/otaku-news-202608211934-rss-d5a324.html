Details (EN)

``Pig's Pearl,'' a commemorative work by director ``Issun Saku wa Oji,'' which won the Grand Prix at ``TikTok TOHO Film Festival 2024 (hereinafter referred to as ``TTFF 2024''), has been released on the official TikTok of ``Tik Tok TOHO Film Festival'' and the official YouTube of ``Tik Tok Japan.'' This vertical short animation depicts a world where cuteness and unease intertwine, with the main character being Chika, a girl played by actor Kyoko Saito.

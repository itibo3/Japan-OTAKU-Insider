Details (EN)

The TV anime *Kagurabachi* will begin airing in April 2027. It has been announced that the second leg of the world tour, designed to spread excitement for the series, will take place in Johannesburg, South Africa, on September 27 (local time) and in Melbourne, Australia, on October 11 (local time). The first 20 minutes of Episode 1 will be screened at conventions in each location.

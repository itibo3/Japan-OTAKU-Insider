It has been decided that the exhibition "MAPPA EXPO 15th Anniversary" will be held to commemorate the 15th anniversary of the establishment of animation studio MAPPA. It will be held at "YURAKUCHO MUSEUM" in Yurakucho, Tokyo for approximately 80 days from Wednesday, September 16, 2026 to Monday, December 7, 2026.

Nadare Takamine's original character "Sophia F. Schirling" is now a 1/6 scale figure in a slightly clumsy magician outfit! A deluxe version is also available that allows you to express the figure with the costume exposed.

Summary

Overseas hobby manufacturer "Partylook" will release the figure "Sophia F. Schirling Magician Bunny Ver." in May 2027. The price is 33,000 yen for the regular version and 34,100 yen for the deluxe version.

Details (EN)

A collaboration café featuring “Detective PreCure!” and Sweets Paradise will be held at three COLLABO PARADISE locations from November 26 to December 27, 2026. The event will feature a total of five food and dessert items inspired by the Pretty Cure characters, as well as drinks that will rotate between the first and second halves of the event, and merchandise featuring illustrations exclusive to Sweets Paradise.

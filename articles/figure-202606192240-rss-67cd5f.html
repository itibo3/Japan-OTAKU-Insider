Japanese title

プラモデル「1/24 シャコタン☆ブギ」シリーズから「道秋のケンメリ」、「京子のジャパン」が10月に発売予定

Summary

Aoshima Bunka Kyozaisha will release plastic models ``1/24 Shakotan Boogie No.06 Michiaki no Kenmeri'' and ``1/24 Shakotan Boogie No.07 Kyoko's Japan'' in October. The price is 3,520 yen each. Reservations will be accepted from 2:00 pm on June 26th.

Anime “Bocchi the Rock! '' comes Cospa's ``ankle socks'' and ``metal carabiners,'' which are decorated with each of the band members, including Hitori Goto, Nijika Ijichi, Ryo Yamada, and Ikuyo Kita. Reservations are now being accepted at Cospa official shops, including mail order, and some products will also be pre-sold at the 2D Cospa booth in "Comic Market 108" to be held at Tokyo Big Sight on August 15th and 16th, 2026.

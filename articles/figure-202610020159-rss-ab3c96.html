Japanese title

「トランスフォーマー スタジオシリーズ」でサウンドウェーブ、オプティマスプライム、サイクロナスが2027年3月下旬発売！

Summary

Takara Tomy will release three Studio Series products from its “Transformers” line of action figures in late March 2027. The prices are 4,950 yen for “TS-44 Soundwave” and 7,150 yen each for “TS-45 Cyclonus” and “TS-46 Optimus Prime.”

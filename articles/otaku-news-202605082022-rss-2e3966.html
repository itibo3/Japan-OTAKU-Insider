A collaboration project has begun between six Kodansha manga works, ``Blue Period,'' ``Blue Rock,'' ``Cells at Work!,'' ``Medalist,'' ``Attack on Titan,'' and ``Ace of Diamond,'' and Waseda Academy, which runs a cram school for elementary, middle school, and high school students. Eshin Jinpachi from ``Blue Rock'' and Levi from ``Attack on Titan'' decorate the new advertisement for the summer seminar, and new visuals and web movies are currently being released.

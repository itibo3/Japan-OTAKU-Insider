Details (EN)

A total of eight collaborative ramen products inspired by the four schools from the anime *Haikyu!!*—Karasuno High School, Aoba Josai High School, Otonokoma High School, and Fukurodani Academy—are now available. Starting November 12, 2026, these products will be available for purchase on the online store “Takumen.com,” and limited-time dine-in events will also be held at four venues.

Details (EN)

"Reze/Bom" from the theatrical film *Chainsaw Man: Reze Arc* is being released as a 1/4-scale figure. This item is produced by "Prime 1 Studio," known for its high-end sculpting, and is priced at 238,590 yen (tax included) for the DX edition and 187,990 yen (tax included). The release is scheduled for May 2028, and pre-orders are currently being accepted at “AmiAmi,” a major online hobby retailer.

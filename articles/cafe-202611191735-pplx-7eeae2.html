To commemorate the 10th anniversary of the GiGO Collaboration Cafe Akihabara Building 3, the menu and merchandise for the first installment of the "Love Live! Series" collaboration cafe, "Nijigasaki Academy School Idol Club," have been announced. The first installment will run from November 21 to December 17, 2026, and will feature drinks inspired by the 12 members as well as parfaits representing the four units. Reservations will be made via a lottery system on the GiGO app.

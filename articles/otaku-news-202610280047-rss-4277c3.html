It has been revealed that TV Tokyo, as part of a new initiative in the anime industry, will collaborate with Plott—a company that plans, produces, and develops IP content centered on short anime—to launch the short anime series *Mecha Samurai*. Hiroyuki Takei, known for *Shaman King*, is serving as the main character designer for the series, which will begin streaming on YouTube and other social media platforms in November 2026.

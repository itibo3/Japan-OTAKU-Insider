Details (EN)

HD remastered versions of 14 carefully selected titles from Toei Video’s OVA label, “Toei V Anime,” will be available exclusively on a subscription-based, all-you-can-watch basis on “d Anime Store” starting October 10, 2026. This initiative commemorates the label’s 40th anniversary and features a lineup ranging from well-known titles—including *Teito Monogatari*, *Desert Rose: The Rose of Sand, The Apocalypse of Snow*, *Elf-17*, and *HEN*, which were available from day one—to hidden gems that have never been released on any format other than VHS.

Details (EN)

The 33rd movie in the “Crayon Shin-chan” series, “Crayon Shin-chan the Movie: Mysteries!” "Ola's Yokai Vacation" will be released on July 31, 2026. The guest voice actors for this work have been announced, and it has been revealed that Sari Ito will be playing the nine-tailed fox monster "Yako". Comedy duo Mayurika will also appear as the Edomae Sushis, the monsters that stand in the way of Shinnosuke and his friends.

"Jujufes 2026 -5th Anniversary-," the finale event marking the 5th anniversary of the *Jujutsu Kaisen* anime, was held at K Arena Yokohama on August 29 and 30, 2026. This article features Junya Enoki (Yuji Itadori), Emi Ogata (Yuta Okkotsu), Yuma Uchida (Megumi Fushiguro), Asami Seto (Nozomi Kugisaki), Mikako Komatsu as Maki Zen’in, Kenjiro Tsuda as Kento Nanami, Takahiro Sakurai as Kei Natsume/Kensaku, Koji Yusa as Naoya Zen’in, Tomokazu Sugita as Hiromi Hikuruma, and Tomoyo Kurosawa as Takada-chan.

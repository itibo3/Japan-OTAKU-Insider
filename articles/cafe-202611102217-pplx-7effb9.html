Details (EN)

To commemorate the “Hetalia 20th Anniversary Original Art Exhibition: WorldFesta” being held at Namba Parks in Osaka, a limited-time collaboration café will be open from November 7 to November 23, 2026, in the museum café space inside #702 CAFE&DINER Namba Parks. The café will offer a menu inspired by the series and its characters, distribute free gifts to customers, and provide limited-edition placemats as part of a joint campaign with the mixx garden Osaka Nipponbashi branch.

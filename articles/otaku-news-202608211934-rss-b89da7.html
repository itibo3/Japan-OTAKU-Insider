It has been announced that the ``Happy Set,'' which comes with ``Puppet Soonsun'' and ``Bread Robber'' toys, as well as a mini illustrated book of ``Pokémon,'' will be sold at McDonald's nationwide, excluding some stores, for a limited time from August 28, 2026.

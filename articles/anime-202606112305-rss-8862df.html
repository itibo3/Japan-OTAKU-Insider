Details (EN)

The presentation ceremony for the 30th Osamu Tezuka Cultural Prize, sponsored by Asahi Shimbun, was held today, June 11th, at Asahi Hall in Yurakucho, Tokyo. At the 30th edition, Ao Kojima's ``The more you sell a book'' won the Manga Award. The New Life Award went to Saito Mado for ``Dissecting a Monster,'' the short story award went to Kazujiro Kawajiro's ``A New Friend: Kawajiro's Short Story Collection,'' and the special award went to Kazuyoshi Takeda for ``Peleliu: Guernica in Paradise.''

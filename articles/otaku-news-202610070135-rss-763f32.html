Details (EN)

New “Chikawa” merchandise includes the “2027 Year-Themed Items,” featuring a fantastical night sky, as well as “belly warmers” and “1-minute-length belly warmer pants”—perfect for the coming season—and “gift envelopes” that can be chosen based on purpose and design. The “2027 Year-Themed Items” will go on sale sequentially starting October 16, 2026; the “belly warmers” will be available in early October, the “gift envelopes” in late October, and the “1-minute-length belly-warmer pants” in late November.

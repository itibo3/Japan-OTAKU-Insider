Details (EN)

The original anime *Tsuku no Ko 2: Re-Bond*, featuring Toa Gosei’s “Aron Alpha” instant adhesive, was released on August 26, 2026. It serves as a sequel to *Tsuku-no-ko*, which was released on November 29 of last year as part of the “Stick-Together Youth Project” and depicted a “youthful Aron-dol story where dreams and glue come together.” One of the highlights is that it was intentionally created as a “poorly animated anime,” and the cast includes Kana Asumi, Kotaro Nishiyama, Misa Ishii, and Hana Ogawa.

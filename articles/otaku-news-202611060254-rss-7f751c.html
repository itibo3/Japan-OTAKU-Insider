Details (EN)

The Netflix animated series *Fool's Night* will premiere exclusively worldwide on November 26, 2026. The key visual and main promotional video have now been released. Additionally, Tempalay has been confirmed as the artist for the opening theme, and Nowlu as the artist for the ending theme. It has also been revealed that the cast for additional characters will include Shion Wakayama, Dai Tsuchida, Nobuhiko Okamoto, Ayaka Senbongi, and Yumiko Kobayashi.

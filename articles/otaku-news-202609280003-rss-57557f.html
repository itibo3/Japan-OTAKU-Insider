Details (EN)

A collaboration between *Touken Ranbu ONLINE* and the “Special Edition Castle EXPO in Kumamoto 2026” has been announced. The event will feature panel displays of six Tōken Danshi—including Kasen Kanesada, Hotarumaru, and Dōtanuki Masakuni—who are associated with Kumamoto and Kumamoto Castle, as well as a stage event starring “Okkii Konnosuke” and the sale of collaboration merchandise.

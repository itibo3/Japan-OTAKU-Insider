Details (EN)

VISION8 announced today (August 10, 2026) that it will hold an exhibition titled ``-40th Anniversary of Debut- Kosuke Fujishima Original Art Exhibition'' to commemorate the 40th anniversary of manga artist and character designer Kosuke Fujishima's debut. It will be held at Yurakucho Marui from September 18th to October 4th and Namba Marui from October 30th to November 15th. Admission fee is 1500 yen (tax included).

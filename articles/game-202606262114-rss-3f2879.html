Details (EN)

Tatsumaki Games today released the PC game ``CozyMining''. This work is a soothing idle game that resides on the desktop. When you click on the destination, the character moves automatically and digs underground while collecting ore and treasure chests. There are 20 types of ore, ranging from copper to orichalcum, and you can forge a pickaxe with the materials you collect.

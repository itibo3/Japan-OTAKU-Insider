On September 30th (Wednesday), the special effects puppet show series ``Thunderbirds'' will celebrate its 60th anniversary since it began broadcasting on television in Japan. To celebrate, it has been revealed that various anniversary measures will be carried out, including the production of a special 60th anniversary key visual for the Japanese version, the development of commemorative products, a collaboration project in VR space, and a special screening and talk show to celebrate "Thunderbird Day" on September 30th.

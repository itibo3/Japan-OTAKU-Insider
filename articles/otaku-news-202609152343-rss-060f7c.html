From *Jujutsu Kaisen*, a rubber stamp set featuring line art silhouettes of the characters’ full bodies, titled “Jujutsu Kaisen Line Art Stamp Collection (Kaigyoku & Tamori Ver.),” is now available. The lineup features a total of 8 designs: Satoru Gojo, Kei Geto, Shoko Ieiri, Kento Nanami, Yu Haibara, Meimei, Riko Amagai, and Jinharu Fushiguro.

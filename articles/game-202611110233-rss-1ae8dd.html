On November 10, 2026, GoldFireStudios announced that “Ranchonauts” will be released on Steam sometime in 2027. The game is a “space Western co-op action” title in which an explorer, stranded on an unknown planet after their spaceship was destroyed, herds a flock of “space sheep” and travels from town to town to earn money to cover the enormous repair costs.

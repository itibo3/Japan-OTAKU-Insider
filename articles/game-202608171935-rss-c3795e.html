On August 16, 2026, COOL&CREATE began distributing Touhou Project's derivative game ``Internet Survivor Survivors'' on Steam. This work is a roguelite action game in which you control Flandre Scarlet and complete the stream by defeating enemies and increasing the number of viewers. Strengthen your weapons with likes and coins and survive with the aim of reaching 1 million registered users.

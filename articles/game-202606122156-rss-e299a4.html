Square Enix today announced that new goods "Dragon Quest Weapons Collection Vol. 1" will be released on September 4, 2026. This is a collection figure that is a 1/12 scale three-dimensional version of the weapons and shields that appear in the "Dragon Quest" series. A total of 6 types are available, including Loto's sword and Dragon Killer.

System Soft Beta announced today that Season 2 of “Grand Strategy SSB2” will begin on November 26, 2026. The company will release the "Power-Up Kit" DLC—which includes a "Campaign Mode" featuring 30 maps to conquer—as well as weapon, map, and BGM packs for "Australia" and "India," and will also roll out a major free update for the main game.

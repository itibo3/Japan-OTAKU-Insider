The movie "Resident Evil" will be released in movie theaters nationwide from October 9, 2026. It has now been decided that the Japanese dubbed version will be screened, and it has been revealed that Hiro Shimono will play the main character, the delivery man Brian, and Saori Hayami will play the role of Brian's girlfriend Michelle. Along with this, a preview of the Japanese dubbed version was also released, which begins "Tsutunai Ichiya" with Shimono's screams.

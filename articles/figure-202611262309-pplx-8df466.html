Details (EN)

BANDAI SPIRITS will begin accepting pre-orders for the second shipment of the "S.H.Figuarts Ultraman Victory Night" action figure on Premium Bandai starting at 4:00 p.m. on November 27. The set includes the Night Timber and Shepherd Saber, allowing fans to recreate the dual-wielding pose. Priced at 9,900 yen, it is scheduled for shipment in July 2027.

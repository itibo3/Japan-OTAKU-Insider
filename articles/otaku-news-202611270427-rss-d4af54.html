To commemorate the 25th anniversary of the launch of the comprehensive Gundam magazine *Monthly Gundam Ace*, framed full-color reproductions of original artwork and black-and-white reproductions of original manuscripts—part of the “Gundam Ace 25th Anniversary” collection—are now available.

Details (EN)

"Winter Journey," a themed café based on the TV anime *Freiren: The Funeral March*, will open for a limited time in Tokyo and Osaka starting December 17, 2026. The café will feature a plate of soup and a "magic book" sandwich inspired by the show’s winter setting, as well as foot-bath lattes and newly illustrated merchandise.

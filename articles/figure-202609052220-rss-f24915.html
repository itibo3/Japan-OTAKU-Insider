Details (EN)

To commemorate the 9th anniversary of the release of "Azur Lane" in Japan on September 14th, "AZUR LANE MUSI9 FES." will be held on September 5th and 6th at the Shinjuku Sumitomo Building in Shinjuku, Tokyo. This event will feature a variety of projects to commemorate the 9th anniversary, including a stage event where you can enjoy live performances and talks, as well as a "Sound Museum" themed around the "sound" of the same title.

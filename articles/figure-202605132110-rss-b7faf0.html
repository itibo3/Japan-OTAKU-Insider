Hobby Japan's latest product in the HJM (HJ MODEL KIT) series, ``NSR50,'' has finally been completed, and the completed product was exhibited as a set with a runner at the company's booth at the Shizuoka Hobby Show. It is scheduled to be released from September onwards, and the price is 3,960 yen. At the booth, we were able to take a close look at the painted finished product, so we would like to show you its charm.

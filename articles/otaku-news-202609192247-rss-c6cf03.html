Details (EN)

At the “Kyoto International Manga & Anime Fair (Kyomaf) 2026,” a special stage event titled “TV Anime ‘Detective Conan’ 30th Anniversary Stage: *Heart-Pounding! A Crimson-Hued Opening!*” was held, featuring Ryo Horikawa (voice of Heiji Hattori) and Yuko Miyamura (voice of Kazuha Toyama). When Horikawa and Miyamura appeared dressed in kimonos, the audience erupted in loud applause. Miyamura, in a yellow kimono and a ponytail—a nod to the character Kazuha she voices—brought the house down.

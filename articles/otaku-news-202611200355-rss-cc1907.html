Details (EN)

It has been announced that the TV anime *New Oishinbo* will begin airing sequentially starting January 4, 2027. To mark this occasion, a main promotional video and key visual have been released to vividly celebrate the start of this new chapter. Furthermore, additional cast members—Daisuke Ono, Yasuaki Takumi, Akane Fujita, and Tomokazu Seki—have been announced to portray key characters who hold the keys to both turbulent romantic entanglements and the world of fine dining, and their comments have been released.

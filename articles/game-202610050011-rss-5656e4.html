Details (EN)

The Kinki Bureau of Economy, Trade and Industry views the gaming industry as a growth sector for the region and has begun efforts to foster an environment conducive to the creation of new game IPs in the Kinki region. The first initiative in this series brought together six individuals: Masafumi Kimura of room6, Masakazu Matsushita of HexaDrive, Shunji Mizutani of PLAYISM, Masahiko Murakami of SkeletonCrewStudio, Zenryu Mori of Osaka Electro-Communication University, and Koya Yokota of Directors’ Univ. to host a public session on the theme of “Game Development” at OIGS2026.

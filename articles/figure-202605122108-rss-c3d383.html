Japanese title

三重県桑名市にカプセルトイ専門店「ガチャガチャの森　アピタ桑名店」が5月15日オープン。ユニー商業施設に初出店778台のカプセルトイマシンを展開

Summary

Luluark will open a capsule toy specialty store, ``Gachagacha no Mori Apita Kuwana Store,'' on the second floor of the commercial facility ``Apita Kuwana'' in Kuwana City, Mie Prefecture, on May 15th. Business hours are from 9:00 to 21:00.

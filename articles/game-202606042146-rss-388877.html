City Connection today began distributing the additional aircraft DLC "R-9AixExtraArrow" for "FZ:FormationZ" for the Switch2/PS5 version. The aircraft is modeled after the R-9, the main character in the R-TYPE series, and is equipped with familiar armaments such as the robot form and the Force. The PC/XBOX Series X|S version will be distributed from 23:00 today.

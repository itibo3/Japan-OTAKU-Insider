Details (EN)

The “Yumeiro Patissière POP-UP SHOP in Marui” will be held at two locations: Yurakucho Marui starting Friday, November 27, 2026, and Ueno Marui starting Saturday, December 12, 2026. The event will feature a pre-release sale of numerous new merchandise items featuring brand-new illustrations by Natsumi Matsumoto based on the “Maid & Butler” concept. Additionally, venue-exclusive purchase bonuses, a stamp rally, and an exhibition of standee panels are planned.

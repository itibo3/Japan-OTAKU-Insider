On November 6, 2026, KUROGAMES announced that it will release a major update, Ver. 3.8, titled “The Lily Wilting in a Faded Dream,” for its open-world action RPG “Narushio” on November 12. This update will kick off a new story arc—Chapter 4, Act 5 of the Tidal Missions, titled “A Bouquet Dedicated to Tomorrow”—and introduce the new ★5 Resonator, “Lily.”

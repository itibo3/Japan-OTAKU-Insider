Japanese title

「勇者王ガオガイガー」よりプラモデル「HG 撃龍神」3次発送分がプレバンにて5月18日11時より予約開始

Summary

BANDAI SPIRITS will begin accepting reservations for the 3rd shipment of the plastic model ``HG Gekiryujin'' on the mail order site ``Premium Bandai'' from 11:00 on May 18th. Shipping is scheduled for June, and the price is 7,150 yen.

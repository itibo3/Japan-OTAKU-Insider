Details (EN)

A collaboration café between the theatrical anime *Expelled from Paradise: Resonance of the Heart* and ORBcafe will be held from November 13 to December 11, 2026. The admission fee is 550 yen and includes a placemat. The café will feature limited-edition collaboration menu items inspired by the anime, as well as promotional items and original merchandise.

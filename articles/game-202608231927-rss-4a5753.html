On August 22, 2026, Kyoto Entertainment Works released the iOS version of the fireworks simulation app ``Become a Fireworks Craftsman: Night Sky Atelier.'' Basic play is free, and you can purchase the one-time ``Pro Unlock'' that unlocks all features for 700 yen (tax included). This work is a new version of the series that started in 1996 and has been remade for modern smartphones.

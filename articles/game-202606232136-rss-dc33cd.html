Details (EN)

Nexon today (June 23, 2026) announced the establishment of a public-private fund to foster early-stage independent studios in South Korea. The partnership, consisting of NexonPartners, KonaVenturePartners, and the Korean Ministry of Culture, Sports, and Tourism, will invest a total of 250 billion won (approximately 26.3 billion yen) over the next five years to invest in startup companies in stages.

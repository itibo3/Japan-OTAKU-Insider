SoftSource announced today that it will release the physical edition of the mountain city-building simulation “Laysara: Summit Kingdom” for PS5 and Switch on February 4, 2027. In this game, players build towns amidst rugged mountains, prepare for natural disasters such as avalanches, and develop a logistics network connecting multiple towns.

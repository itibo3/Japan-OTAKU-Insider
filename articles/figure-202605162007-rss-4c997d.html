漆黒の機体はリサイクルの証。「MG 1/100 ユニコーンガンダム [リサーキュレーションカラー/クリアネオングリーン]」が再生産【ガンダムベース撮り下ろし】

Summary

As part of the "Gunpla Recycle Project" advocated by BANDAI SPIRITS, the limited item "MG 1/100 Unicorn Gundam [Recirculation Color/Clear Neon Green]" that uses recycled materials has been reproduced and is now on sale at Gundam Base.

Details (EN)

As part of the "Gunpla Recycle Project" advocated by BANDAI SPIRITS, the limited item "MG 1/100 Unicorn Gundam [Recirculation Color/Clear Neon Green]" that uses recycled materials has been reproduced and is now on sale at Gundam Base.

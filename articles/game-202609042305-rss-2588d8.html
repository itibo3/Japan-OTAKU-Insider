Details (EN)

BS Asahi’s official game label, “AMBERGAMES,” has decided to publish the new game “HEIMA,” developed in collaboration with KENEIDESIGN. The game is scheduled for release on PC in 2028. In this “immersive spatial puzzle-action” game, players take on the role of a boy searching for his missing older brother as he travels through various parallel worlds.

Toei announced today (August 28, 2026) that its gaming brand, “Toei Games,” will make its debut appearance at the “Tokyo Game Show 2026.” In addition to showcasing special playable demos of “HINO,” “KILLA,” and “DEBUGNEPHEMEE” at the booth, the latest playable demos for “DEBUGNEPHEMEE” and “HINO” are scheduled to be released on Steam on September 10 and September 17, respectively.

Japanese title

ガシャポンオンラインにて「機動戦士ガンダム CAPSULE INDEX 04」が7月15日11時より予約開始

Summary

Bandai will begin accepting pre-orders for the Gashapon ``Mobile Suit Gundam CAPSULE INDEX 04'' at the online store ``Gashapon Online'' from 11 a.m. on July 15th. Shipping is scheduled for late November, and the price is 400 yen per shipment.

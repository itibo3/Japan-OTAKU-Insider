Koikoi's 1/6 scale figure "Koikoi -Sakura- Umise Aoba" is scheduled to be released in October 2026. This is a three-dimensional version of ``Aoha Kaise'' who appears in ``Aoha Kamise Wants to Be Only for You'' drawn by illustrator Ururihi.

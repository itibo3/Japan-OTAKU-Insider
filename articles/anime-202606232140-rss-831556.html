Japanese title

「釣りキチ三平」複製原画など展示「矢口高雄の世界展」が東京アウトドアショーで

Summary

18 original reproductions of Takao Yaguchi's ``Tsurikichi Sanpei'' will be exhibited at the ``Tokyo Outdoor Show 2026,'' which will be held from June 26th at Exhibition Halls 1, 2, and 3 of the Makuhari Messe International Exhibition Center in Chiba.

Details (EN)

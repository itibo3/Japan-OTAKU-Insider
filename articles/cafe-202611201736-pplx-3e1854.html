Details (EN)

A collaboration café called “Retro Pop Café,” based on *Girls & Panzer: More Love-Love Operation!* and Shabera Café, will open sequentially at three locations in Tokyo, Nagoya, and Osaka starting December 11, 2026. The event will feature three food items, six drinks, and merchandise based on newly drawn illustrations of the six characters in retro-pop outfits. Reservations for visits will be accepted starting at 6:00 PM on November 20 via a paid deposit system.

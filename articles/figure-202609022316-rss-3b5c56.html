Details (EN)

Tamiya has announced that the 64th All Japan Model Hobby Show will be held in 2026. The venue is Tokyo Big Sight South Halls 3 and 4. The event will be held from October 2nd to 4th, with October 2nd being trade day. Admission tickets on general days are 1,500 yen. Advance tickets are 1,100 yen. Advance tickets will be sold at "livepocket" below, and you will need to register/log in to the service to purchase. Free for junior high school students and younger.

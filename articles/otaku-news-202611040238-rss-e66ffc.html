It has been announced that “Girls Band Cry THE MOVIE,” a brand-new feature film based on the original anime series “Girls Band Cry” produced by Toei Animation, will be released on May 21, 2027. To mark the occasion, a teaser visual and a teaser trailer have been released, and it has also been announced that the first batch of “Muvichike” advance tickets (cards) featuring a newly drawn visual will go on sale on November 6.

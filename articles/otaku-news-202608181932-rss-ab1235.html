Details (EN)

A screening event with a stage greeting to commemorate the release of the Blu-ray & DVD of ``Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba the Movie: Mugenjou Chapter 1: The Resurrection of Aquarium'' was held on August 16, 2026 at TOHO Cinemas Shinjuku, Screen 9 in Tokyo. At the venue, Hiro Shimono, who played the role of Zenitsu Agatsuma, who plays the brother disciple, and Yoshimasa Hosoya, who played the role of Keitake, took to the stage under the MC of producer Yuma Takahashi.

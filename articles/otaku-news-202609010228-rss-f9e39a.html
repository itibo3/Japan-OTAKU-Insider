"TorePortal," a fan app that integrates the exchange, sale, and management of merchandise related to favorite idols, has launched an official partnership with "streamart," an e-commerce platform specializing in merchandise for content creators. By making it easier to manage and trade merchandise acquired on “streamart” within “TorePortal,” this initiative aims to foster an environment where fans can connect with greater peace of mind while preserving the unique excitement of random merchandise.

Details (EN)

It has been announced that the light novel ``The Strongest Magician's Reclusion Plan'' will be made into a TV anime and will be broadcast on the ``Agal Anime'' slot of 28 CBC/TBS networks nationwide in 2027. Along with this, a teaser visual and teaser PV depicting the main character Ars Regin, played by Yuto Uemura, were released for the first time. Additionally, some staff information was revealed, and congratulatory comments and illustrations from the original authors were also received.

The official tournament for Supercell’s smartphone multiplayer game “Brawl Stars,” the “Brawl Stars World Championship 2026,” will kick off on November 20, 2026, at the Tokyo Metropolitan Gymnasium. This tournament, the first of its kind to be held in Japan, will feature 12 teams—including defending champion CrazyRaccoon and East Asia’s top-ranked team, ZETADIVISION—competing for a total prize pool of $1 million and the title of world champion.

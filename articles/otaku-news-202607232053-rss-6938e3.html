Details (EN)

A collaborative project by the female VTuber group Hololive, ``Flolife Healing Bath Tour ~Hitomeguri'', will be held at 11 stores of the Gokurakuyu and RAKU SPA Group from August 6, 2026. The event will feature Choco Yuzuki, AZKi, and Nene Momosuzu, and will include a collaboration menu where you can receive collaboration goods and coasters, and a collaboration bath with a scent chosen by the talent.

Details (EN)

The second Nintendo Switch version of the smartphone game app ``Hypnosismic -Alternative Rap Battle-'' ``Hypnosismic -Alternative Rap Battle- 2nd period'' will be released on July 16th. It has been decided that the theme song for this work, "Diggin'", will be released on various music distribution services from midnight on July 16th. Furthermore, it was announced that the music video will be released on Hypnosis Mic's official YouTube channel at 7pm on the same day.

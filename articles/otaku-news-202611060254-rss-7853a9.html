Volume 1 of Reiya Machida’s first manga collection, *Kanon Master* [AA], went on sale on the 4th. According to DMM Books’ comic description, the story is set in “a land of sand where ‘Magic Statues’—giant weapons forged in past battles—threaten the people.” The back cover describes it as a “comedy action series about a master and apprentice in a world of sand and wind,” while the obi tagline reads, “A quest to defeat giant statues of wind and sand, woven by a master and his apprentice...”

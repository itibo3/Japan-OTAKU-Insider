Details (EN)

From the anime *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba*, a pair of platform loafer sneakers featuring decorations inspired by the tsuba of the Nichirin Blade has been released. They are produced by “MAYLA,” a fashion brand known for its tagline “Your body temperature rises by 2°C,” and models featuring “Tanjiro Kamado,” "Giyu Tomioka," "Kyojuro Rengoku," "Tengen Uzui," "Muichiro Tokito," and "Miya Fushigawa" are currently available for pre-order.

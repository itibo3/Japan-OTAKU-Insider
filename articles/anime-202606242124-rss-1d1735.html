A pop-up shop commemorating the release of the novel ``Mobile Police Patlabor Sushi Restaurant Goto'' based on the anime ``Mobile Police Patlabor'' will be held from June 25th to July 5th on the 2nd floor of Shinjuku Marui Annex in Tokyo.

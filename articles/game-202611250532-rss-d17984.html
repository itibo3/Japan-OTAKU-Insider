Details (EN)

Game developer "Mr. Sakuchau" released the free browser game "Make the Stone Round and Roll It" on November 20, 2026. In this game, players carve granite into a round shape at a small local factory, roll it down a slope reminiscent of Showa-era Japan, and compete to see how far it travels. It can be played on PCs and smartphones without the need for a download or account registration.

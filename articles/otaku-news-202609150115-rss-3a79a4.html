Details (EN)

"Kurama's Guide to Beautiful Hair," a campaign by Rasana Hair Care's premium line "Premior," which focuses on wavy hair for adults and is a collaboration with the TV anime *YuYu Hakusho*, has launched. Kurama has been appointed as a special instructor to guide fans toward beautiful hair. A web commercial has been released, and a giveaway campaign has begun on the brand’s official social media channels. Furthermore, starting September 15, a limited-quantity “Luxury Bonus Set That Even the Demon Realm Would Envy,” featuring original merchandise with newly drawn illustrations of Kurama and Fox Spirit Kurama, will go on sale.

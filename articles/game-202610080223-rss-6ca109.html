Details (EN)

I played the first closed beta test (CBT) of *Astrae Oratio*, a new fantasy magic RPG developed by Dynamis One. In this article, I’ll introduce the story where “magic” and “civil service” intersect, the unique turn-based battle system that makes full use of counters and substitutions, and the “Free Time” feature where you can interact with other mages. While the game’s stunning visuals are certainly appealing, there are also areas regarding battle balance and game flow that we hope will be refined in future updates.

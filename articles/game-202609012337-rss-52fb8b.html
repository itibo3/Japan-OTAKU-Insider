Details (EN)

Yokozuna Games today released the Steam store page for the PC game ``Tactful Tactics.'' This work is a simulation RPG that supports cooperative play for up to 6 people. The concept is to ``turn a turn-based strategy into an online cooperative game,'' and the aim is to create an experience similar to ``online cooperative digital chess.''

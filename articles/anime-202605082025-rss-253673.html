Details (EN)

Volume 1 of Ryo Amei's ``Lunch Bancho Hitomi'' based on the original work by Masayuki Kusumi will be released on May 25th. To commemorate this, Kusumi's talk show and Kusumi and Amei's autograph session will be held at the Nishiogi Building in Tokyo from 1:30pm on June 6th.

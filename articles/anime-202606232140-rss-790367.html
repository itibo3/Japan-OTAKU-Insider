Details (EN)

The second preview of the full-length animated film ``THE RIBBON HERO'' based on Osamu Tezuka's ``Knight of the Ribbon'' has been released. Seiran Kobayashi plays the role of Pine, a friend of the main character Sapphire, Kouki Uchiyama plays the role of Velvet, an executive of the religious organization Mitra, and Mayumi Shintani plays the role of Jirko, Sapphire's old lady. The theme song will be performed by Girls Archives.

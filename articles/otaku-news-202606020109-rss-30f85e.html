Details (EN)

To commemorate the release of the Blu-ray/DVD of ``Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba the Movie: Mugenjou Chapter 1: The Return of Aquarium'', a collaboration project called ``[Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba x Rakuten Group] Blu-ray &amp; DVD Release Commemoration! ``Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba'' Mugen Shopping Team Returns!'' has begun on various Rakuten Group services. If you meet the participation conditions for the target service, you will be entered into a lottery to receive an ``Original Kinchaku Bag'' featuring a collaboration illustration of ``Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba'' and ``Okaimono Panda.''

Details (EN)

Shin-Ei Animation, the animation studio best known for *Doraemon* and *Crayon Shin-chan*, has released a new promotional video featuring updates on the production of new episodes for the TV anime series *New Oishinbo* and *Atashi'nchi NEXT*. The announcement was made during the “Shin-Ei Animation 50th Anniversary” special stage at the “ABEMA Anime Festival 2026,” held on September 19, 2026, as part of the studio’s 50th-anniversary commemorative initiatives.

Details (EN)

The TV anime *New Oishinbo*, set to air in 2027, will feature Yui Makino’s opening theme “PROMISE ME” and Skoop On Somebody’s ending theme “Proof” to enhance the series. The songs featured in the original anime—Megumi Yuki’s “YOU” and Yuma Nakamura’s “Dang Dang Kiniaru”—were urban love songs that, at first glance, seemed an unexpected fit for a story about food. In the new series as well, attention is focused on the romance between Shiro Yamaoka and Yuko Kurita, as officially announced.

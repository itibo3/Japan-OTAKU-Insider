It has been announced that a major exhibition uncovering the “behind-the-scenes stories” of *Jujutsu Kaisen* will be held in Shinjuku, the setting for the series’ final battle. The exhibition will run from Friday, December 25, 2026, to Monday, January 25, 2027. A new key visual, drawn exclusively for the exhibition by Gege Akutami, has also been unveiled.

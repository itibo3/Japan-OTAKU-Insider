Details (EN)

Takara Tomy has released four products from the “Transformers” Studio Series action figure line: “TS-47 Alicon,” “TS-48 Autobot Wheelie,” “TS-49 Autobot Jazz,” “TS-50 Shockwave” from the “Transformers” Studio Series, as well as “AOTP-58 Starscream” and “AOTP-59 Onyx Prime the Deceiver” from the Age of the Prime Series, in late April 2027. The Studio Series figures are priced at 4,950 yen each, while the Age of the Prime series figures are priced at 9,900 yen each. Pre-orders will begin on November 5.

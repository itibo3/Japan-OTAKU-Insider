Details (EN)

At OIGS2026, I played a demo of the 2D exploration action game *The Ninja Who Crosses the Eternal Night*. You ride kunai thrown into walls and use time-stopping abilities to create footholds in midair. Even for me—someone who isn’t great at action games—it was easy to play, and the dashing and jumping felt satisfying. I spoke with the developer, Cass, about the efforts made to refine the game’s feel and the reasoning behind combining kunai with time-stopping mechanics.

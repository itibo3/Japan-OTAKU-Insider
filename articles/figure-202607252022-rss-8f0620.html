Details (EN)

Furyu will release "Yoshitori x F:NEX Shikinami Asuka Langley -Japanese Doll- 1/4 Scale Figure" and "Yoshitori x F:NEX Rei Ayanami -Japanese Doll- 1/4 Scale Figure" as new products of the hobby brand "F:NEX" in July 2027. Made to order, the price is 198,000 yen each. Reservations will be accepted on the hobby e-commerce site "FURYU HOBBY MALL" from July 25th.

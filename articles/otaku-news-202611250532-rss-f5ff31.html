Details (EN)

At “Godzilla Interception Operation,” a popular attraction at “Nijigen no Mori”—a cutting-edge theme park centered on the themes of anime, technology, and nature, located within Hyogo Prefectural Awaji Island Park—the “Godzilla -1.0” Special Exhibition, which was held in 2024 to great acclaim, is currently making a comeback. The exhibition has generated significant excitement, with attendance surpassing 10,000 visitors within the first two months, and the event is set to conclude on December 6—just two weeks from now.

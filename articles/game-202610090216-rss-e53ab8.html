Details (EN)

NEWOLDPIXELS announced that it has signed a licensing agreement with Microids regarding "The Legend of JuJu" (international title: "Toki"). The plan is not to create a remake, but to make the original version playable on game consoles and computers that were not supported at the time of its initial release.

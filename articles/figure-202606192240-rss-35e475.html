Japanese title

「ローゼンメイデン」より「真紅 キャストドール＜特別再販 2026＞」が6月26日に受注開始

Summary

DOLK will begin accepting orders for the cast doll "Shinku Cast Doll " on the e-commerce site "DOLK Online" from 7:00 pm on June 26th. The price is 143,000 yen. Shipping is scheduled to start around July 2027.

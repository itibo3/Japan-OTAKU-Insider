Takara Tomy Arts today announced that two versions of the arcade game ``Onegai Ipuri'' will be released sequentially from June 4, 2026. In the 2nd edition, a new character, Oribia's bazurium coordination, ``Yuki's Flower Bazurium,'' will appear. Additionally, a collaboration with "Sanrio Characters" will be held.

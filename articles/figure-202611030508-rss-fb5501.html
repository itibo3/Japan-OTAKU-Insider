Summary

Taito’s figure brand “spiritale” will release the “Goddess of Victory: NIKKE Viper 1/6 Scale Figure” in July 2027. Pre-orders are scheduled to begin at 11:00 a.m. on November 6 and will run until 11:59 p.m. on December 21. The price is 29,700 yen.

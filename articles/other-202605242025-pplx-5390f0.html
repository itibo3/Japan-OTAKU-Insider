Japanese title

『ストリートファイター6』3周年記念ポップアップストアが東京ソラマチで開催

Summary

GRAPHT will be holding “STREET FIGHTER 6 3rd Anniversary OFFICIAL POP UP STORE in Tokyo Solamachi®” from May 27th to June 16th. The venue will be the wagon shop at 5 Tower Yard on the 3rd floor of Tokyo Solamachi®, and new items will also be on sale.

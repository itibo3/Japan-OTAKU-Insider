Details (EN)

The Game Creators Guild announced today (October 1, 2026) that it will hold the 11th session of the “Game Director Study Group”—a study session for up-and-coming game directors—on October 27 at 404NotFound in Shibuya, Tokyo. The study session will feature Shuhei Yoshida as a guest speaker and will mark the first time this typically closed-door study group has been opened to the public.

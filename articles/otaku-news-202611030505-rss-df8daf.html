To commemorate the 30th anniversary of the *Persona* series, the “Ichiban Kuji *Persona* Series 30th ANNIVERSARY” is scheduled to go on sale on November 28, 2026. The lineup includes figures of the protagonists from *Persona 3*, *Persona 4*, and *Persona 5*, as well as an Effectone that plays “The Poem of Everyone’s Soul” when the door opens and closes.

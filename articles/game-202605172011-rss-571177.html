Details (EN)

Yostar announced today (May 16, 2026) that it will be distributing a program for the smartphone app "Azur Lane" and will hold an in-game event "Alliance in Front of the Holy Seal" from May 20th until maintenance on June 12th. Along with this, new ships of the Iron-Blooded faction will be implemented, including the UR battleship "Götz von Berlichingen" and the UR submarine "U-2501."

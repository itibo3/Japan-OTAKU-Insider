Details (EN)

Fujimi Models will begin shipping the plastic model kits “1/700 Toku 205 Imperial Japanese Navy Carrier-Based Aircraft Set 2 (Late War)” and “1/700 Toku 205EX-1 Imperial Japanese Navy Carrier-Based Aircraft Set 2 (Late War)” on November 10. The price for the “1/700 Toku 205 Imperial Japanese Navy Carrier-Based Aircraft Set 2 (Late War)” is 2,200 yen, and the price for the “1/700 Toku 205EX-1 Imperial Japanese Navy Carrier-Based Aircraft Set 2 (Late War)” is 2,860 yen.

Details (EN)

At “WePlay Expo 2026,” held in Shanghai, China, I got to play a demo of *Sleepover*, a cosmic horror adventure game set in a post-apocalyptic world. Set in a world where humanity has vanished, the game depicts the final night spent by two young girls. With its comic-style cut-ins, bold color palette, and poignant portrayal of the protagonist’s psychological state, the game creates a world permeated by a quiet, lingering sense of dread.

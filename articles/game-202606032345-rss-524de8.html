Details (EN)

British game development team Moonana has announced a new title, ``Bathory-Heritage of Blood.'' This work is a 2D action game inspired by ``Castlevania'' in which a vampire princess fights for succession to the throne with her mother. It features a risk-managed combat system in which attacks consume life force and recover by collecting blood from the enemy.

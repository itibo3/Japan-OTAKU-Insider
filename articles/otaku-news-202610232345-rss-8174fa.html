Volume 1 of Miyabi Naruse’s first manga collection, *High-Humidity Mutual Unrequited Love* [AA], went on sale in Akihabara on the 22nd. The obi tagline reads, “100% Humidity & Preciousness! Childhood Friends × Intense Feelings,” while the back cover describes it as “The high-humidity daily life of childhood friends whose relationship keeps getting tangled up just as they’re about to fall in love with each other.” In her release announcement, Miyabi Naruse wrote, “Both the guy and the girl are total crazy ones...”

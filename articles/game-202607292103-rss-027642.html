Hamster announced today that ``Console Archives Doraemon'' will be distributed on July 30th. This title is a reprint of the action game released by Hudson in 1986 for 8-bit home game consoles, and allows you to enjoy an adventure in which Doraemon makes full use of his secret tools and searches for his friends who have been sent to space, the demonic realm, and the ocean floor.

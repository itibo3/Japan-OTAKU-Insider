Details (EN)

Autumn-themed sweets featuring the “Suica Penguin,” the mascot of JR East’s “Suica” card, are now on sale for a limited time and in limited quantities at the “Cake & Bakery Shop” on the first floor of the Hotel Metropolitan in Tokyo. The selection includes the “Chestnut Cake,” featuring a cute, chestnut-shaped Suica Penguin face, as well as “Pudding” and “Seasonal Cakes” made with seasonal ingredients such as pumpkin and pears—the flavors of autumn.

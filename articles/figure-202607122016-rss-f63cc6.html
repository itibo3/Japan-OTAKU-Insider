On July 18th, Tamiya will release wooden dinosaur craft kits using wooden parts, ``Walking Brachiosaurus Craft Set,'' ``Walking Tyrannosaurus Craft Set,'' and ``Walking Triceratops Craft Set.'' The price is 2,750 yen each. These three products are currently on display at Tamiya Plastic Model Factory Tokyo.

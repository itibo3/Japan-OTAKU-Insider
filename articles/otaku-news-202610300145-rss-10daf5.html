Details (EN)

It has been announced that a collection by the fashion brand “WIND AND SEA,” created in collaboration with the tokusatsu TV series “Ultraman”—which is celebrating its 60th anniversary this year—will go on sale starting October 31, 2026. Actor Shiro Sano, who has ties to the series—including an appearance in the latest installment, *Ultraman Theo*—will serve as the look model for the collection, which features a total of 16 styles that reinterpret the Ultra Heroes and popular monsters in a street-style aesthetic.

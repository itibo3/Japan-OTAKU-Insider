Volume 1 of Futaro’s first collected volume, *The Elf Who’s Been Repeating the Same Year for 100 Years* [AA], went on sale on the 20th. The comic’s description reads, “A pitifully pathetic appearance, unlike that of a mysterious and beautiful elf.” The back cover features the tagline, “The weakest, cutest, scrawny elf,” along with “Lots of laughs, with a touch of heart,” while the obi reads, “With the weakest, hopelessly cute elf...”

Details (EN)

At the WePlay 2026 event being held in Shanghai, China, we got a sneak peek at “QUIET,” currently in development by LINE Games. This cooperative horror game follows a group of duck-like aliens who have crash-landed on Earth as they steal parts needed to repair their spaceship from an elderly woman’s mansion. The old woman reacts not only to footsteps and other sounds but even to voices spoken directly into the microphone.

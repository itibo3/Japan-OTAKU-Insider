Details (EN)

KUROGAMES announced today (August 7, 2026) that it will begin distributing Ver. 3.6 ``Shadow of the Illusionary Clouds, Worldly Melancholy and the Heart of the Sword'' on August 20th on the open world action RPG ``Narushio'' that is currently in service. In this new story, the wanderer meets the legendary sword immortal ``Seyoi'' and the mysterious guest ``Kagenen'' who crosses both the Yin and Yang worlds, and takes on the challenge of a new mystery.

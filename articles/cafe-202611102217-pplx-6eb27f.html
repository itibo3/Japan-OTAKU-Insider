Details (EN)

A collaboration café featuring “Saint Seiya” and Chugai Grace Café will be held at the Shibuya and Osaka locations from December 3 to December 30, 2026. Merchandise featuring newly drawn illustrations of the 12 Gold Saints will be available, along with collaboration menu items such as Big Bang Curry and the Fire Clock Glass Cake. A coaster will be given as a gift with every order.

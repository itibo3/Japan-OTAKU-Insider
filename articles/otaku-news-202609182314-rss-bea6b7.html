Details (EN)

To celebrate the exclusive Prime Video unlimited streaming release of the movie *Chainsaw Man: Reze Arc*, “Kenshi Yonezu Station”—a channel featuring a 24-hour nonstop stream of Kenshi Yonezu’s music videos and live performances—is open for a limited time on YouTube. Viewers can enjoy a wide variety of videos, including the music video for “IRIS OUT,” the movie’s opening theme, and his performance on the “Kohaku Uta Gassen,” as well as videos for “JANE DOE,” “KICK BACK,” and “Lemon.”

Details (EN)

NetEase has announced a new smartphone game ``Beneath the Mist'' and will conduct the first test from August 17th to 23rd, 2026. This work is an escape-type action ADV in which you and your friends explore an area covered in fog, collect ingredients and treasure, and aim to retreat. Its unique feature is that by cooking and eating the monsters it has subjugated, its power can be acquired.

Details (EN)

Teikoku Databank conducted an industry survey targeting anime production companies based on the credit report file "CCR" containing 2 million companies and other external information. This is the 11th similar survey since August 2025. Although the production market exceeded 400 billion yen for the first time, it was pointed out that it may have peaked out. It was also predicted that production capacity would be limited due to a labor shortage, and the market was expected to shrink for the first time in five years in 2026.

Details (EN)

To commemorate the third anniversary of the anime *Freiren: The Funeral March*, a “3rd Anniversary Anime: Season 2 Highlight Screening” was held on September 26, 2026, at TOHO Cinemas Hibiya in Tokyo. Following the screening of a specially curated selection of episodes from the second season of the TV anime, a stage greeting was held featuring Atsumi Tanezaki (voicing Freiren), Kana Ichinose (Fern), Chiaki Kobayashi (Stark), Tarusuke Aragaki (Genau), and Reina Ueda (Metode) took the stage for a greeting; the event was also broadcast via live viewing to eight other theaters.

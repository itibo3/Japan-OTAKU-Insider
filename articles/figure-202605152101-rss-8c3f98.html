At the Good Smile Company/Max Factory/Wave joint exhibition booth at the 64th Shizuoka Hobby Show, Max Factory's new series ``Rider's portrait'', which will be developed at ``PLAMAX minimum factory'', was exhibited. This is a collaboration item with Honda Motor Co., Ltd., and is a plastic model that combines the company's motorcycles with figures depicting their riders.

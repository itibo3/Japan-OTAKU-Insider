Details (EN)

The monthly series “My Collaboration Café,” published on the fourth Saturday of every month, is a column in which Tetsuya Onishi—a working chef—creates his own collaborations with various video games and other sources, then shares the recipes inspired by them. This installment’s theme is the giant block of tofu that speaks in a Kansai dialect, as seen in *Resident Evil 2*. He even went so far as to use the food enzyme transglutaminase to create a block of tofu capable of surviving in Raccoon City.

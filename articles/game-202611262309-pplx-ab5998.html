Nexon has rolled out a major update for the online RPG *Mabinogi Mobile*. New additions include the lightning-element magic class “Electro Mage,” Chapter 2 of the main story, “The Witch of the Wilderness,” and the “Enchant” enhancement system. A new raid, “White Succubus,” is also scheduled to begin on December 7.

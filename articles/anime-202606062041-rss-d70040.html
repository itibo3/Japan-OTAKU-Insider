Japanese title

アニメ「あかね噺」展が7月から渋谷で開催、作品の魅力を立体的に体感できる

Summary

The ``TV Anime ``Akane Banashi'' Exhibition'', an exhibition of the TV anime ``Akane Banashi'' based on the original work by Hiroki Suenaga and Takamasa Umagami, will be held at HMV museum Shibuya 6 in Tokyo from July 11th to August 9th.

The TV anime "Sayonara Lara" is currently airing on TOKYO MX and other channels. An interview with Director Takashi Koide and his staff regarding the series’ opening (OP) sequence has been published in the “Case Studies” section of IMAGICA Entertainment Media Service’s official website. The interview reveals the behind-the-scenes story of the production of the OP’s analog segments, in which they took on the challenge of creating “authentic cel animation.”

Summary

The "LEGO Store Nagashima" in Kuwana City, Mie Prefecture—a LEGO brand store—will reopen following renovations on October 29, and the "LEGO Store Yokohama Landmark Plaza" in Yokohama City, Kanagawa Prefecture, will reopen on November 10.

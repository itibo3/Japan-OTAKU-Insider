Furyu will release the “Nachoneko Birthday Ver. 1/7 Scale Figure” from its hobby brand “F:NEX.” The price is 29,975 yen. Pre-orders will be accepted starting at 10:00 a.m. on October 11. The pre-order period runs until 11:59 p.m. on December 4. The product is scheduled to ship in June 2027.

Details (EN)

In addition to information on new products from manufacturers, there is another big highlight on the general days of the Shizuoka Hobby Show. That is the "Modelers Club Joint Exhibition." This is the largest exhibition in Japan, where modelers from all over the country gather, and in addition to the vast space that occupies half of the south building of Shizuoka Messe, their works will be crammed into a large number of venues: the exhibition hall on the 2nd floor of the west building, the exhibition hall on the 3rd floor of the north building, and the reception hall on the 4th floor of the north building.

Details (EN)

When you think of the Hakone Tozan Railway, the image of trains running through the mountains along a series of steep gradients and sharp curves is particularly striking. Among them, older cars—whose origins trace back to the trains from the line’s opening days—are still in active service today. These are the “100 Series” trains, characterized by their traditional, rounded body design. It has been announced that the 100 Series will be retired in January 2028.

A collaboration between *Expelled from Paradise: Resonance of the Heart* and *Armored Trooper Votoms: The Gray Witch* has been announced. Given that their release dates are just one week apart and that both are robot anime, a “joint battle visual” featuring Scope Dog and Ahan has been unveiled. It was also revealed that “joint battle standees” will be displayed at 30 locations nationwide starting October 29.

Azone International will start accepting reservations for the dolls ``1/6 Pureneemo Character Series No. 170 ``The Idolmaster'' Makoto Kikuchi'' and ``1/6 Pureneemo Character Series No. 171 ``The Idolmaster'' Yukiho Hagiwara'' from May 22nd.

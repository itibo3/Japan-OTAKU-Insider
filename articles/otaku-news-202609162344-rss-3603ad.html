Details (EN)

The first-ever exhibition commemorating the 10th anniversary of the TV anime *Re:Zero—Starting Life in Another World*, titled “TV Anime *Re:Zero—Starting Life in Another World* Exhibition -10th Anniversary-,” has been running at the Shibuya BEAM Gallery in Tokyo since September 12. On September 11, the day before the opening, a media preview and press conference were held, featuring Yusuke Kobayashi, who voices Subaru Natsuki, and Rie Takahashi, who voices Emilia. They got an early look at the exhibition, which encapsulates the 10-year journey of “Re:Zero,” and shared their impressions, memories of the voice recording sessions, and insights into rare audition recordings.

Details (EN)

System Soft Beta announced today that it will release an update on August 27, 2026, at 10:00 a.m., adding the scenario map “Battle of Mogadishu” and the free map “Three Arrows” to *Grand Strategy SSB2* at no cost. Players will be able to enjoy a scenario featuring a clash between the Turkish military and “Al-Shabaab,” as well as a free map in which North Korea, South Korea, and China form an alliance to challenge the United States.

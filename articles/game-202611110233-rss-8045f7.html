Details (EN)

2PGames today released the competitive party game “WorstClassEver” on Steam. In this game, up to eight players are divided into teachers and students and compete to see who can pull off the most pranks during class. According to the developer, the demo took first place in the player count rankings at the Steam Next Fest in October and has surpassed 1 million cumulative downloads.

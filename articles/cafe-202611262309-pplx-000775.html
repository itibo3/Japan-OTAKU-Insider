Details (EN)

A 10th-anniversary collaboration café for the TV anime *Aikatsu Stars!*, themed around “Colorful Check,” will be held sequentially starting January 16, 2027, at three locations—Shinjuku, Nagoya, and Shinsaibashi—at the Shafu Café. Original merchandise featuring newly drawn illustrations of Yume, Laura, and four other characters will also be on sale.

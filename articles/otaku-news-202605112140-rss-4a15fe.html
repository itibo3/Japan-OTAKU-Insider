Details (EN)

In response to Nintendo Co., Ltd.'s latest financial results and business outlook, the company's stock price fell sharply on the 11th, dropping below 7,000 yen at one point. Immediately after the start of trading, the price hit a low of 6,895 yen, setting a new low since the beginning of the year. After that, the price remained around 7,000 yen at the end of the market, and the closing price of the day was 7,000 yen.

Details (EN)

The first exhibition to commemorate the 10th anniversary of the anime "One Punch Man", "Anime 10th Anniversary 'One Punch Man Exhibition'" will be held from June 26th to July 20th, 2026 at the event space in Sunshine 60 Observatory Tenbou Park in Ikebukuro, Tokyo. In preparation for the event, a key visual drawn by character designer Sei Kubota has been released. Exhibition details, merchandise information, and information on the collaboration menu offered at Aitenbou Park CAFÉ have also been revealed.

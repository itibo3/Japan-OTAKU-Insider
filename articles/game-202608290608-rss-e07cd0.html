At gamescom 2026, currently being held in Cologne, Germany, a playable demo of “GodSaveBirmingham,” developed by OceanDriveStudio, was on display. The game is set in 14th-century England, which has been devastated by zombies. It combines action that takes full advantage of Unreal Engine 5’s physics simulation with a rich life simulation.

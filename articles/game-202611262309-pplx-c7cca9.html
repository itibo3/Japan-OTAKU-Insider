Toei Animation has released “Expelled from Paradise: The Stellar Angel,” a brand-new visual novel that serves as a sequel to the theatrical anime “Expelled from Paradise,” on Nintendo Switch and Steam. The game features the “K2-SYSTEM,” which allows the story to branch based on player choices, and the Steam version is available at a 10% discount for a limited two-week period to celebrate its release.

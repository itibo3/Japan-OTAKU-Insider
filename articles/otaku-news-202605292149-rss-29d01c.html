The TV anime "Yuri!!! on ICE" will celebrate its 10th anniversary this year since its first broadcast in October 2016. A 10th anniversary visual drawn to commemorate this occasion has been released. Furthermore, it has been decided that a Blu-ray & CD BOX containing the main TV series, past bonus footage, songs, etc. will be released. In addition to this, it has also been revealed that a large-scale exhibition event is scheduled to be held this winter.

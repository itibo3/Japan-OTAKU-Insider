Details (EN)

The event for *Re:Zero—Starting Life in Another World*, titled “*Re:Zero—Starting Life in Another World* POP UP STREET in Okuyama O-mairi-machi and Asakusa Nishi-sando Shopping District” will be held from October 2 to October 25, 2026, at the Okuyama O-mairi-machi and Asakusa Nishi-sando Shopping District in Asakusa. The event aims to liven up the shopping district with a variety of activities, and original merchandise featuring collaboration illustrations will be available for pre-sale at a special pop-up shop.

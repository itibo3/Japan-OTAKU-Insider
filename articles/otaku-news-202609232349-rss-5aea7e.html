Details (EN)

It has been announced that “and GALLERY” will host a collaboration café titled “The Movie ‘Kitaro: The Birth of GeGeGe’ × and GALLERY Collaboration Café,” based on the film *The Birth of Kitaro: The Mystery of GeGeGe*. Featuring original illustrations themed around the “Moonlit Night Party Café,” the café will be open for a limited time from October 3 to October 31, 2026, at and GALLERY’s Yurakucho, Namba No. 2, and Nagoya No. 1 locations.

The winners of the 50th Kodansha Manga Award have been decided. The boys' category was awarded to Kei Ura's ``Gachiakuta'', the girls' category was awarded to Ari Shirakawa's ``My return from death at a magic school life, starting with my ex-lover and the prologue (*However, the likability rating was zero)'', and the overall category was awarded to Shun Umezawa's ``Darwin Incident''.

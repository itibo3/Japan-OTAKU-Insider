Norsfell announced its new title, “Aftergods,” today (October 9, 2026). In this top-down survival action RPG, you play as the child of a god who survived Ragnarok, gathering followers to build a new pantheon. You’ll use divine powers to survive, such as gathering resources through storms and summoning temples.

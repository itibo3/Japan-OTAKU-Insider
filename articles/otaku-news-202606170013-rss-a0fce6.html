The latest TV anime in the Ghost in the Shell series, Ghost in the Shell: THE GHOST IN THE SHELL, will be broadcast from July 7, 2026 on Kantele/Fuji TV's nationwide network on Tuesdays at 11:00 pm in the Tuanibaru!! slot. We have recently learned that this work will be available for unlimited domestic viewing on Prime Video from 11:30 pm on the same day. Along with this, a trailer video for the Prime Video version was also released.

Individual developer Jeriko announced today (May 29, 2026) that he is holding an open play test for his new title "Gambit" which is currently under development. This work is a roguelike chess game where you challenge yourself to play chess with rules that change every time. Anyone can participate in the playtest by requesting access from the Steam store page.

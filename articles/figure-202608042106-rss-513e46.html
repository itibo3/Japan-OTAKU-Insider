Summary

KADOKAWA will release the plastic model "KADOKAWA PLASTIC MODEL SERIES 'Spice and Wolf MERCHANT MEETS THE WISE WOLF' Holo" in January 2027. The price is 9,680 yen for the regular version and 12,980 yen for the DX ver.

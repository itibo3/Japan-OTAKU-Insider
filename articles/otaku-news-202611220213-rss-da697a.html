Details (EN)

"Freiren: The Funeral" is a manga currently serialized in Shogakukan's "Weekly Shonen Sunday," written by Kaneto Yamada and illustrated by Tsukasa Abe. Set in the world “after” the Demon King was defeated by the Hero and his party, the story follows the journey of Freiren—a sorceress over a thousand years old who fought alongside the Hero to defeat the Demon King—and the new people she meets along the way.

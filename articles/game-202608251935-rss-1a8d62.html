Details (EN)

Nippon Television announced that it will air the film “Exit 8” for the first time on terrestrial television as part of its “Friday Roadshow” program on August 28, 2026, at 9:00 p.m. This live-action film is based on the video game of the same name, which was released on November 29, 2023. The film grossed over 5.1 billion yen at the box office, and this broadcast will feature the full, uncut version of the movie.

Details (EN)

A campaign to commemorate the release of the remastered versions of the love adventure games ``The Prince of Tennis'' and ``Doki Doki Survival'' (hereinafter referred to as ``Gakupuri'' and ``Dokisaba'') will be held at Lawson stores nationwide (*excluding some stores, Natural Lawson, and Lawson Store 100) from May 19, 2026 (Tuesday). When purchasing eligible products, there will be a project where you can receive an original clear sheet on a first-come, first-served basis in limited quantities, a quotation post campaign, and the sale of original goods.

Details (EN)

An exhibition commemorating the 30th anniversary of the “Evangelion” series, titled “30th Anniversary Exhibition: ‘ALL OF EVANGELION,’” will be held at Hirakata Park, an amusement park in Hirakata, Osaka, from October 18, 2026, through January 11, 2027. To coincide with this, it has been announced that a collaboration project will be held on the Keihan Electric Railway starting on October 18, the first day of the exhibition. In addition to the sale of limited-edition commemorative tickets, a commemorative stamp rally will be held, and specially decorated trains featuring commemorative headmarks will also run.

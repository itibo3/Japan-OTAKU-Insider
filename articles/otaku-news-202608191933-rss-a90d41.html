Details (EN)

The anime ``SEKIRO: NO DEFEAT'', based on the game ``SEKIRO: SHADOWS DIE TWICE'', will be shown in theaters nationwide for a limited period of three weeks from September 4, 2026. This time, a new scene cut showing the beautiful background art of this work, which was entirely hand-drawn in 2D, has been unveiled. Furthermore, on August 18th, which is ``Rice Day'', the character information of Ochinomiko was also revealed, and a new visual was received.

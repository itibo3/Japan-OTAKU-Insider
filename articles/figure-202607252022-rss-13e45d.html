Details (EN)

MEDICOM TOY's ``MEDICOM TOY 30th ANNIVERSARY EXHIBITION'' is being held for six days from July 25th to July 30th at Space O on the third basement floor of the Omotesando Hills main building in Omotesando, Tokyo. Admission is free. This year marks the 30th anniversary of the company's establishment, and valuable BE@RBRICKs from the past are also on display. Of course, as always, there were many new works from MAFEX and soft vinyl on display.

Volume 9 [AA] of *Conqueror of the Doomed Land*, a manga adaptation of Futeori-ke’s novel illustrated by Masashi Sabekomura, officially went on sale on the 8th. The comic’s promotional text reads, “Yuri and his companions, attacked by the ‘Ruler of the Skies’ and the ‘Dragon’s Sudden Assault,’ crash-land in enemy territory,” while the cover tagline states, “Outwit fate amidst the looming threat of death.”

Details (EN)

It has been announced that the new movie *Ultraman Theo: The Movie—The Counterattack of Menesias*, based on the currently airing *Ultraman Theo* TV series—the latest entry in the *Ultraman* franchise—will be released on February 23, 2027. Along with the announcement, details were revealed about “Meneas,” an assassin targeting Theo—a new character in the film—and the new monster “Aremegas,” along with a teaser trailer. A comment was also received from special guest cast member Masashi Kasamatsu, who will help weave the story of this film alongside the main cast of the TV series.

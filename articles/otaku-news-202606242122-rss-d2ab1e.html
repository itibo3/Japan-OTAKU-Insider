Details (EN)

To celebrate the 50th anniversary of the monthly girls' manga magazine "LaLa" published by Hakusensha, it has been decided to hold an exhibition titled "50th Anniversary LaLa Original Art Exhibition - Fascinating Creations". The Tokyo venue will be held from July 17, 2026, and the Osaka venue will be held from September 5, 2026.

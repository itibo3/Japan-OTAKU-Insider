NEOLAVI began distributing a demo version of its PC game, “Aren’t You Curious About What’s Inside Your Favorite VTuber?” today. In this document-screening adventure, players take on the role of a rookie security guard tasked with manning the entrance to the VTuber agency “Stellaria,” where they must decide whether to grant or deny visitors entry.

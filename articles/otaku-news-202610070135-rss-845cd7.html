Details (EN)

A limited-edition “Sugu-Umai Cup Miso Soup” series featuring characters such as Tanjiro Kamado has been released as part of a collaboration between the anime *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba* and miso and fermented food manufacturer “Hanamarki.” It has been announced that all six varieties will begin shipping sequentially starting in mid-October 2026. Additionally, to commemorate this collaboration, Hanamarki’s official X (formerly Twitter) account will host a giveaway featuring prizes such as an “original acrylic stand set” and a “complete box set.”

Details (EN)

Atlus announced on May 22, 2026 that "PersonaSoundStationVol.2" will be distributed on the official YouTube channel of the company's game sound label "ATLUSGAMEMUSIC" from 18:00 on May 29th to 0:00 on May 31st. In this project, songs such as ``Megami Ibunroku Persona,'' ``Persona 2 Sin,'' and ``Persona 2 Punishment'' will be distributed for 30 hours.

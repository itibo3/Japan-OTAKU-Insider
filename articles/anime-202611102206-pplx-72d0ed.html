Details (EN)

"Harajuku Himegoto House," a brand-new original short anime series for terrestrial TV produced by Plott, Bandai Namco Music Live, and Animate, has kicked off, with the release of a teaser PV, the music video for the first song, and character and cast information. This media mix project depicts the chaotic antics of seven “cute assassins” working at a café in Harajuku, featuring a cast that includes Nobuhiko Okamoto, Shotaro Morikubo, and Yuya Hirose.

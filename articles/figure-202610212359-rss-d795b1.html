HIKO7 will release its die-cast model “1/64 Toyota CROWN Athlete (2005)” in four colors—White Pearl Crystal Shine, Black, Silver Metallic, and Ginger Red Mica Metallic—at its flagship store, “Model Gallery HIKO7,” and at model shops nationwide starting in December. The price for each is 4,620 yen.

THQNordicJapan today announced that early access for the historical RPG "Expeditions: Samurai" will begin on Steam on August 7, 2026. This work is set in Japan at the end of the Warring States period, and the protagonist moves from Europe to Japan, builds bonds with eight friends, and carves out history as he makes numerous decisions. A new trailer introducing the friends has also been released.

Details (EN)

Two special-edition ramen products featuring designs from the anime *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba* will go on sale nationwide starting September 15, 2026. The two varieties are “Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba Tonkotsu Ramen” and “Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaibi Tantanmen.” Each box contains one serving and comes with a random pair of original chopsticks from a set of six designs.

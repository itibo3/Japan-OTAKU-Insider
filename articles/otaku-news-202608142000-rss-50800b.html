Details (EN)

The TV anime "Bungo Stray Dogs" is celebrating its 10th anniversary since its start on TV in 2026. To commemorate this, screenings of the 2nd season ``Black Era'' edition, the 3rd season ``15 Years Old'' edition, and the 4th season ``The Secret Story of the Establishment of the Detective Agency'' edition will be held in six cities nationwide (Tokyo, Hokkaido, Kanagawa, Aichi, Osaka, and Fukuoka).

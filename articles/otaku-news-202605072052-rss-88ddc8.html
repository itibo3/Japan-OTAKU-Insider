It has been announced that the TV anime ``World is Dancing'' will be broadcast from July 2, 2026. Along with this, the main PV and key visual, where you can also hear the opening theme "Shuuyoi" sung by Macaroni Pencil, have been released. Additionally, additional cast members Takahiro Sakurai, Nobuo Tobita, Mamiko Noto, Inori Minase, and Hazuki Seto have been announced, and comments have arrived.

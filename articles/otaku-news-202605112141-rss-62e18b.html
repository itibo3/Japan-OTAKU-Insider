Details (EN)

It has been revealed that the manga ``Umi ga Runu End Roll'', which won first place in the female section of ``This Manga is Amazing! 2022'', will be made into an anime movie and will be released in 2027. The animation will be produced by Kyoto Animation under the direction of Taichi Ishidate. Along with the announcement, a special announcement and visuals for the animated film were unveiled, as well as comments from director Ishidate and original author John Tarachine.

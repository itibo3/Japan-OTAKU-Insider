Details (EN)

To commemorate the 25th anniversary of the manga ``BLACK LAGOON'', it has been decided that a POP UP SHOP ``BLACK LAGOON 25th ANNIVERSARY ~Top 3 Scariest Women on Earth~ POP UP SHOP'' will be held. Starting on September 12, 2026, it will travel to five venues nationwide, starting with the Grand Entas venue in Akihabara, Tokyo, and will also feature goods drawn by original author Rei Hiroe.

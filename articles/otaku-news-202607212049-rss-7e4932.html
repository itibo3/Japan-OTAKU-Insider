Details (EN)

It has been announced that the TV anime ``Pokémon'' will air a collaboration episode with the game app ``Pokémon Sleep'' titled ``Wenival and Dekanu-chan, Operation Sleep!'' on August 21, 2026. The story depicts Riko and the Rising Voltechers heading to the main island of Wakakusa, the setting of "Pokémon Sleep," and Dr. Neroli, played by Natsuki Hanae, also appears.

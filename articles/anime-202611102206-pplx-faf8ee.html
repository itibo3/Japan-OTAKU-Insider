Details (EN)

It has been announced that the fantasy series *My Death Flags Know No Bounds*, which has sold over 1.75 million copies in total, will be adapted into a TV anime in 2027. To mark the announcement, celebratory comments from the original author, Izumi-sensei; character designer A-chiki-sensei; and manga artist Mitsuya Otosu-sensei were released, along with a commemorative illustration by Otosu-sensei.

Awatreasure has announced that the “Engage SR3 Late Model” plastic model kit from “Five Star Stories” will be re-released three years after its initial release!

Japanese title

アワートレジャー、「ファイブスター物語」よりプラキット「エンゲージSR3 後期型」が初版から3年を経て再販決定！

Summary

Our Treasure will re-release the "Engage SR3 Late Model" plastic model kit in March 2027. The standard edition is priced at 8,580 yen, the Vibra Chorus! Edition at 16,500 yen, and the version with a metal nameplate at 9,900 yen.

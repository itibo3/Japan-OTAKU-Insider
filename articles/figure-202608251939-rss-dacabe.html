Aoshima to Release 1/24-Scale Kit of Liberty Walk's "Aventador GT Evo" in December

Published: 2026-08-25
ID: figure-202608251939-rss-dacabe
Category: figure

Japanese title

アオシマ、リバティーウォークの「アヴェンタドール GT Evo」を1/24スケールでキット化、12月発売

Summary

Aoshima will release the 1/24-scale plastic model “1/24 Liberty Walk No. 26 LB Silhouette Works Aventador GT Evo Ver. 2” in December. The price is 7,260 yen. Pre-orders will begin on September 1 at 2:00 PM.

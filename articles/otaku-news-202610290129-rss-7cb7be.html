Details (EN)

Taito announced the results of a survey on “fandom activities” and “merchandise” conducted in conjunction with “Good Fandom Day” on November 4. When asked which “oshi-katsu” merchandise they thought would be popular in 2026, “identity mascots” were voted number one. Additionally, “plush toys (small and medium)” took the top spot for the third consecutive year as the most popular merchandise for one’s favorite “oshi.”

Details (EN)

Nippon Television's charity program ``24 Hour TV 49-Love Saves the Earth-'' will be broadcast on August 29th and 30th, 2026. Prior to this, from July 1, ``Chari T-shirts'' designed by Gosho Aoyama, a manga artist known for ``Detective Conan'', will be available for sale, continuing from last year. There are also many other charity goods with the same design, such as clear files and face towels.

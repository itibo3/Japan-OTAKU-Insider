Details (EN)

Takara Tomy will release six products from the AOTP series as part of its “Transformers” line of action figures in late March 2027. The prices are 7,150 yen each for “AOTP-53 Gazelle” and “AOTP-54 Cyber Shark,” “AOTP-55 Skyguard,” “AOTP-56 Autobot Breakaway,” and “AOTP-57 Alpha Bravo” at 4,950 yen each, and “AOTP-EX Power Links Red Alert & Minicon 8-Pack” will be 14,080 yen.

"Baggage Belt," a pixel-art airport idle game that depicts "life behind the scenes at the airport," will be released on Steam on December 3. A free demo is now available.

Japanese title

Summary

A gentle, heartwarming story about running an airport, starting from a small airfield where a worn-out turboprop plane and a Corgi chief steward await, and expanding its routes beyond the Moon and Saturn to a terminal among the stars.

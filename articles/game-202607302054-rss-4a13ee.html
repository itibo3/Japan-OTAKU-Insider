Details (EN)

CynicalHoney today announced that it will release the PC game "SummerSnow." in the summer of 2027, and released the Steam store page. This work is an adventure game that aims to escape from a ``repetitive day'' that suddenly started one day. The story depicts the summer vacation of a second-year high school student, and the story progresses to the next day by interacting with childhood friends and family and avoiding tragedy.

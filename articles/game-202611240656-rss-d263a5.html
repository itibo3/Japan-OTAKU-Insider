At the indie game event “WePlayExpo 2026,” held in Shanghai, China, on November 21 and 22, Poland’s Stability Games showcased “Dreampainters,” a first-person puzzle game for PC. In this game, players apply paints—each with different abilities depending on their color—to walls and floors as they make their way through the painter’s dreams.

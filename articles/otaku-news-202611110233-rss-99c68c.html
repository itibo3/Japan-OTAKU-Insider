Volume 13 [AA] of Riaaki Kotoba’s manga series *The Princess Knight Becomes a Barbarian’s Bride* went on sale on the 9th. The back cover reads, “Safely returned from the ‘Eternal Spring Marshes’… or so it seemed, but the spoils of the journey spark an unexpected uproar!” while the obi tagline states, “In both love and battle, your greatest enemy (rival) is yourself!”

A new flight jacket has been released as part of a collaboration between *Mobile Suit Gundam SEED FREEDOM* and the military brand “ALPHA INDUSTRIES.” Created by “STRICT-G,” an apparel shop that incorporates the world of Gundam, the jacket will be available for pre-order on “Premium Bandai,” the official online store of the Bandai Namco Group, starting at 1:00 PM on November 20, 2026.

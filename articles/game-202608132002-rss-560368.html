Details (EN)

Lasenguru has announced that ``Fate/Grand Order'' will hold a collaboration event ``Mystery Singularity FINAL'' with ``Real Escape Game'' in three cities: Tokyo, Nagoya, and Osaka from December 2026. This event will be held as ``Mystery Singularity FINAL'' by completely updating the contents of ``Mystery Singularity III FINAL'' and following the scenarios of the past two games.

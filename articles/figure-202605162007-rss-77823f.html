Details (EN)

The Shizuoka Model Teaching Materials Cooperative held the general public day of the event ``64th Shizuoka Hobby Show'' on May 16th and 17th. General users will be able to enter from May 16th, and not only will they be able to see newly released products from each manufacturer, but they will also be able to purchase merchandise and venue-limited items. Reservations were required to limit the number of visitors on general days, but as soon as registration began, the number reached the limit. Visitors cannot enter the venue without a QR code obtained in advance, but the moment it started, Kuroyama was filled with people.

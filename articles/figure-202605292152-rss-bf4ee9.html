Details (EN)

The special exhibition "Qualia 10th Anniversary Exhibition" commemorating the 10th anniversary of the establishment of the capsule toy maker "Qualia" will be held for a limited time from May 29th to June 22nd at PARCO FACTORY on the 7th floor of Ikebukuro PARCO Main Building. At this event, in addition to exhibits of past capsule toys, photo spots, and advance sales of new products, there will also be valuable exhibits that show the manufacturing process, including prototypes.

On July 11, 2026, COLOPL announced ``Shironeko Project INFINITY'', a new action game in the ``Shironeko Project'' series. Compatible models are NintendoSwitch2 and NintendoSwitch, and will be released in 2027. More than 50 characters have appeared since the smartphone version, and the new game is centered around ``Character Link,'' which allows you to combine the friends you've collected.

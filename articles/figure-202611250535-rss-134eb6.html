Details (EN)

The film *Armored Trooper Votoms: The Gray Witch (Hekse)* was released on November 20. This film is part of Sunrise’s 50th-anniversary commemorative series, and marks the debut of director Mamoru Oshii on a Sunrise project. Produced by a team consisting of Sunrise and Production I.G. for animation, with music by Kenji Kawai, this is the first part of a two-part series.

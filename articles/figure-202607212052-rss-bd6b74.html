Hasegawa has unveiled three military models that will be released in October. The prices are 6,820 yen for the Japanese Navy A-class destroyer Nowaki 'Battle of the Marianas', 2,860 yen for the 'Messerschmitt Bf109G-6 'Hartmann'', and 5,390 yen for the 'V-22 Osprey 'JGSDF 107th Squadron Saga Special 2026'.

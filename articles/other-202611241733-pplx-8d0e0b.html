Details (EN)

Universal Studios Japan has announced that a collaboration with the anime *The Apothecary’s Tale* will take place as part of “Universal Cool Japan 2027” from February 10 to June 30, 2027. The event will feature the series’ first-ever entertainment restaurant, “The Apothecary’s Musings: Mystery Restaurant—The Mystery of the Floating Dining Hall,” as well as a story-based ride.

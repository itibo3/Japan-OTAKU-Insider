Hakusensha today (June 12, 2026) started a campaign for free all episodes except the latest issue, in conjunction with the update of the latest episode of the manga ``Berserk'' currently being serialized in the company's Young Animal magazine. It will be held in conjunction with the latest episode, which will be updated continuously on June 26th and July 10th, and will be held for a limited time from 0:00 on June 12th to 23:59 on the 13th.

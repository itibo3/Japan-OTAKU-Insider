Details (EN)

CRITICALREFLEX released the open-world adventure game “TANUKI: Pon’s Summer” today. In this game, players take on the role of “Pon,” a tanuki mail carrier, and enjoy making deliveries and interacting with residents while riding a BMX bike across the islands of Japan. The game features over 100 types of BMX challenges and a variety of activities, and players aim to rebuild a shrine using the rewards earned from these activities.

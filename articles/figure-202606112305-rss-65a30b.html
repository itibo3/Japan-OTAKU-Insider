Details (EN)

Hobby Japan will release die-cast painted finished products "Baribari Densetsu 1/24 Completed Bike Koma-gun / Honda CB750F Late Round Mirror" and "Baribari Densetsu 1/24 Completed Bike Koma-gun / Honda CB750F Early Round Mirror" in September. The price is 4,290 yen each. Reservations began on June 11th at the directly managed store ``Post Hobby'' and at model stores nationwide.

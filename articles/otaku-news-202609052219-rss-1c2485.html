The conveyor belt sushi chain "Kura Sushi", which is currently holding a collaboration project with "Chiikawa", announced on its official website on September 5th that the project will be discontinued from the start of business on September 6th, 2026. It was held for a limited time from August 21st, and was popular as the fourth collaboration, but the company said it was due to misconduct by an employee.

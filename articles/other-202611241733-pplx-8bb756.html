A pop-up shop commemorating the 25th anniversary of *Digimon Tamers* and the 20th anniversary of *Digimon Savers* will be held at Grand Entas Akihabara from November 21 to December 6, 2026. Original merchandise featuring newly drawn illustrations themed after each series will be on sale, and simultaneous online sales will be available through Kadost.

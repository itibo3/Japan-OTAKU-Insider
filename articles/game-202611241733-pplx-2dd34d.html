Hirohasu, an independent game developer, announced that he will release the PC game “Mahjong Kart” on January 8, 2027. It is a party game for up to four players that combines mahjong and kart racing, in which players control mahjong tiles to race around a mahjong table and complete their hands. The price is 680 yen.

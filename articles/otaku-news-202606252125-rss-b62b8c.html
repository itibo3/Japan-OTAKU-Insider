Details (EN)

TV anime “Is it a landmine?” Jihara-san” will be broadcast on MBS/TBS 28 stations “Super Animeism TURBO” from January 2027. Furthermore, additional cast members Maaya Uchida, Tomokazu Sugita, and Daisuke Ono were also announced, and comments have arrived. Prior to the broadcast, it has also been decided that a music project by Mai Jihara, the heroine of this work, will be launched.

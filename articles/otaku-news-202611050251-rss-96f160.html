Details (EN)

"TOKYO ANIME NEXT," a prelude event for a new anime award set to launch in 2027, was held in the Ikebukuro area from October 30 to November 2. This article reports on the main event, which took place on November 2—the final day—at Tokyo Tatemono Brillia HALL. The event featured a diverse program ranging from a talk session with Toshiyuki Itakura (Impulse), Shoko Nakagawa, and Sally Amagi, to a live performance by the Tokyo Philharmonic Orchestra, singing performances by Mai Kuraki, Nami Nakagawa, and Ai Higuchi, and the announcement of the new anime awards.

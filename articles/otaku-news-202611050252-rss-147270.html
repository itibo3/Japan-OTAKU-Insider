Details (EN)

It has been announced that the “2027 Mos Burger Lucky Bag,” a collaboration between Mos Burger and the anime “Haikyu!!,” will be sold in limited quantities at Mos Burger locations nationwide, with the exception of certain stores. The set contains four types of merchandise featuring original, newly drawn designs that are suitable for adults to use in their daily lives, and special online pre-orders will begin at 3:00 PM on November 25, 2026. Additionally, this winter will see the introduction of “Moss Meat Buns” and “Moss Wai Wai Sets” that come with original insulated bags created in collaboration with the series, and an in-store announcement featuring the protagonist Shoyo Hinata—voiced by Ayumu Murase—will be played for a limited time.

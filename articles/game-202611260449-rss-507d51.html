StormbirdGames has announced its new PC title, “Deathwake.” This is a Souls-like action game in which a female pirate who has returned from the dead seeks revenge on her former comrades. The game focuses exclusively on boss battles, and a key feature is that the protagonist dies if she takes even a single hit from an enemy.

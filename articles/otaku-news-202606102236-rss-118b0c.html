It has been decided that the limited-time pop-up "HELLO! PUPPET SUNSUN" featuring the character puppet Sunsun, which is currently a hot topic on SNS, will be held. Starting with the Tokyo venue on July 1, 2026, the event will be held in 12 cities nationwide, the largest number ever, including Kyoto, Ishikawa, Saitama, Kagawa, Hiroshima, and Okinawa, which will be held for the first time.

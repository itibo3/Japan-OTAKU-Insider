It has been announced that the first collaboration with Sanrio’s popular character “Pompompurin” will take place at “Sushiro” conveyor-belt sushi chain locations nationwide starting October 7, 2026. A wide variety of merchandise will be available, and “Maze Maze! Purinppe”—a pudding-flavored dish that Pompompurin loves—will also be served.

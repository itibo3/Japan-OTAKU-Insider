Details (EN)

Volume 4 [AA] of Sakane Toyobayashi’s manga series *Futari Bus* went on sale on the 18th. The back cover features the tagline: “Living life as we are, feeling our way through each day. “Starting tomorrow, I’ll take the bus again,” the back cover reads. The manga description reads, “The end of summer and the start of a new school term… Volume 4: Reaching for the Stars,” and the obi tagline reads, “New signs emerge in their time together. A love that wavers as they move forward...”

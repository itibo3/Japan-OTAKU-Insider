Details (EN)

The long-awaited first film adaptation of Chikawa, Chikawa the Movie: The Secret of Mermaid Island, will be released on July 24, 2026. It has been announced that a tie-up project with this work, ``Chiikawa, Parco, Summer.'' will be held at PARCO stores nationwide from July 17th to August 2nd. In addition to original visuals created by art director Asuka Wakita, there will be a variety of other measures to make the event even more exciting, including special novelties for purchases of 5,500 yen (tax included) or more, giveaways on X (formerly Twitter), and a photo booth.

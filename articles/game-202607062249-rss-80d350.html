Frontier Works has announced that the large-scale exhibition ``Hetalia 20th Anniversary Original Art Exhibition WorldFesta'' will be held in Tokyo and Osaka in the fall of 2026 to commemorate the 20th anniversary of the birth of the manga ``Hetalia.'' In addition, the Italian key visual drawn by Hidekazu Himaruya has also been released.

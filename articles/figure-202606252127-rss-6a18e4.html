Details (EN)

Takarajimasha will release ``RecoveryproLab. Fatigue Recovery Wear'', a general medical device that ``supports fatigue recovery just by wearing it'', at 777 GEO stores nationwide from June 27th. Geo stores will have a permanent corner for this product and will mainly sell standard products. Through this initiative, the number of stores handling the product will expand to a total of 2,100 stores.

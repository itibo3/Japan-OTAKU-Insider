``SAKAMOTO DAYS Exhibition'', a large-scale original exhibition of the manga ``SAKAMOTO DAYS'', is currently being held at Gallery AaMo in Tokyo Dome City. Over 170 completed manuscripts and over 90 handwritten creative materials are on display, and Nobunaga Shimazaki, the voice actor who plays Shin Asakura in the TV anime, attended the opening event.

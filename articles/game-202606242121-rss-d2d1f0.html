Details (EN)

Pre-orders have begun for the new game ``Overserved: FoodFighters!,'' which is compatible with a variety of hardware such as NINTENDO64, NEOGEO, Dreamcast, and Sega Saturn. This game, which is said to have been influenced by ``Saturn Bomberman,'' is a party battle game where animals fight using food weapons. On retro game consoles, you can enjoy local battles with up to 8 players.

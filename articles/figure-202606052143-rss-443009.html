Details (EN)

Koedo will hold the grand opening of the indoor survival game field "PHANTOM AVENUE" on June 27th (pre-opening will be on June 5th). The location is Nishihashimoto, Midori Ward, Sagamihara City, Kanagawa Prefecture. The site of the indoor field "Kowloon (Battle City District)", which was once closed and missed by many Sabah gamers, has been completely renovated.

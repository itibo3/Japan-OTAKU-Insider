GamingFactory today announced that the multiplayer feature of the racing game "JDM: Japanese Drift Master" will be implemented for PC (Steam) on June 11, 2026. It supports open world multiplayer where up to eight people can participate in the same session, and features such as voice chat and the ability to place pins on the map are also available.

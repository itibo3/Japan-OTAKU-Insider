Details (EN)

Mikio Igarashi's website ``Bonobono and 40 Years'' commemorating the 40th anniversary of the serialization of ``Bonobono'' and the 10th anniversary of the TV anime broadcast will be officially opened tomorrow, May 17th, in conjunction with ``Bonobono Day''. In addition, it has been revealed that all 500 episodes, which cover 10 years of anime broadcasting, will be distributed for free on FOD, TVer, and YouTube starting at midnight on May 17th, 10 episodes a week for 50 weeks.

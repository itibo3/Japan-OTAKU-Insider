Details (EN)

bilibiliGame today launched the official service for “LumiMaster,” a monster-collecting and -raising RPG. Set in the mysterious and fantastical world of the “Lumia Continent,” this game lets players collect over 100 types of “Rumi” and embark on adventures while raising and evolving them. It also features vertical-screen, one-handed controls and is designed for quick, casual play sessions of about 5 minutes each.

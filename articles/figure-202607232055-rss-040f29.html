Japanese title

初音ミクの派生キャラクター「骸音シーエ」のデビュー15周年を記念した特別仕様のフィギュアが2027年1月再販決定！和装姿の「シーエ JAPANESE Style 15th Memorial Costume」も12月再販決定

Summary

Union Creative will resell the figures "Kokune Cie 15th Memorial" and "Cie JAPANESE Style 15th Memorial Costume" in December and January 2027, respectively. The reservation period is until August 14th at 23:59. The price is 29,700 yen each.

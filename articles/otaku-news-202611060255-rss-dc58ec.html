Details (EN)

It has been announced that *Code Geass: Aspar, the Star-Seeker*, a new anime in the *Code Geass* series, will begin event screenings in 2027. Along with this announcement, the main visual was unveiled, and the main staff, as well as the main cast—including Aiban Shibata and Hibiki Yamamura—were revealed. Furthermore, an introduction to the story—which begins in the year 606 of the Divine Calendar in the Roman city of Neapolis—has also been revealed.

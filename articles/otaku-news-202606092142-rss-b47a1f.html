Details (EN)

The "Puppet Soonsun and Sable Party Campaign" will be held from June 22, 2026 to celebrate the 61st anniversary of Nissin Cisco's "Coconut Sable" by Soonsun from "Puppet Soonsun". Also, from the same day, ``Coconut Sable Melon Pan Flavor'' created in collaboration with ``Puppet Soonsun'' will be on sale in advance at some convenience stores, and will be available at supermarkets nationwide from June 29th.

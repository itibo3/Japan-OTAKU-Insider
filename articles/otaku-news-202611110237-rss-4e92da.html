The Fukuoka leg of the “Winnie-the-Pooh Exhibition: Celebrating the 100th Anniversary of the Original Story” will be held at Fukuoka Mitsukoshi starting February 20, 2027! The exhibition will feature everything from original illustrations to original drawings and background art from the Disney animated films.

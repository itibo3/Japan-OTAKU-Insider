Details (EN)

Phoenixx today announced that "Zashikido Boyfriend I LOVE Ramen" will be released on Steam on September 24, 2026. This work is a monster romantic comedy novel game in which the main character and the zashiki-warashi ``Marilyn'', who aim to open a ramen shop, weave a story while encountering unique monsters. A trial version is available that allows you to enjoy the free public version ``1'' and the beginning of the new part ``2.''

Details (EN)

From Studio Ghibli’s film *Howl’s Moving Castle*, two new items are now available: the “Perpetual Calendar: Calcifer’s Fireplace,” a three-dimensional representation of the fireplace featuring Calcifer, and the “Spinning Color Disc Wall Clock,” which features a mechanism that makes colored discs rotate. They have been available for purchase since November 21, 2026, at “Ghibli Ippai: Donguri Republic” locations nationwide, as well as at the online shop “Donguri Republic Sora no Ue Store.”

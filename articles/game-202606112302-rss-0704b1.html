Today (June 11, 2026), Pictoria, in collaboration with the Beppu City Public Works Bureau, launched a new IP project, ``Hell Furin''. This project is a fusion of Beppu Keirin in Beppu City, Oita Prefecture, and the tourist attraction ``Hell Tour.'' Hiroko Utsumi, known for her work on the anime ``Free!'', is participating in the character design.

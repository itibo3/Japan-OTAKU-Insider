Details (EN)

KRAFTON is showcasing “TARAE: The Unbound” at “G-STAR 2026,” a gaming event currently being held in South Korea. Set in a world based on the Buddhist concept of the Six Realms of Rebirth, this hack-and-slash action RPG lets players mow down hordes of monsters and the undead. The new “Warfiend” class is available for the first time as a playable demo at this event.

Details (EN)

Takara Tomy posted on its official X account that it will unveil new information about “Wataru: Legend of the Demon King” and “Granzoot: The Magic King” from its high-end brand “T-SPARK” at the TOKYO Toy Show 2026. The TOKYO Toy Show 2026 is scheduled to be held at Tokyo Big Sight West Hall, with the trade show taking place on August 27 and 28, and the public exhibition on August 29 and 30.

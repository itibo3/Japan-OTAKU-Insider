Happinet Phantom Studio announced today that the horror film ``Backrooms'' produced by A24 has been decided to be released in Japan. This is a feature-length film by director Kane Parsons that depicts the urban legend ``The Backrooms,'' which spread from a short video on YouTube. It was released overseas on May 29th and became a big hit from the first weekend.

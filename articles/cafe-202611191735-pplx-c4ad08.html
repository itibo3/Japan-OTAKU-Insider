Details (EN)

The collaboration menu and limited-edition merchandise for the TV anime “My Hero Academia” × Animate Cafe have been unveiled. The event is being held from November 1 through November 29 at a total of four locations: Good Smile × Animate Cafe Akihabara and Osaka Nipponbashi, as well as Animate Cafe Nagoya and Kobe Sannomiya. As a bonus for ordering from the menu, customers will receive one random coaster (out of a total of 15 designs).

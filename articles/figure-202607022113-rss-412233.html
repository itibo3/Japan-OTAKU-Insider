Summary

The Japan Toy Association will hold the TOKYO Toy Show 2026 from August 27th to 30th at the West Hall of Tokyo Big Sight. The 29th and 30th are open to the public, and ticket prices are 2,000 yen for one day and 3,600 yen for 2-day tickets.

"Two People Smoking Behind the Supermarket," a story about the two of them enjoying a cigarette in the smoking area behind a supermarket they happened to visit, along with the people around them, will air simultaneously nationwide on 28 TBS affiliate stations starting at 11:56 p.m. on Thursday, July 9, 2026. The series is scheduled to run for 12 episodes.

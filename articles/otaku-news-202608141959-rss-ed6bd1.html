Details (EN)

On the 13th, KADOKAWA Co., Ltd. announced its consolidated financial results for the first quarter (April to June) of the fiscal year ending March 2027. Sales were 68,252 million yen, an increase of 5.3% compared to the same period last year, while operating income was 1,264 million yen, a 45.5% decrease, and ordinary income was 2,490 million yen.

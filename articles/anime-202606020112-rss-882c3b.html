Details (EN)

The main PV of the TV anime "Flame Fighting Ball Girl Dodge Danko" based on the original work by Tetsuhiro Koshitata has been released. It has also been revealed that Sumire Uesaka will play Hako Mikasa, Kanna Nakamura will play Soumi Hiura, Yuka Iguchi will play Juri Igarashi, Satomi Akesaka will play Ryuka Sakamoto, Ai Fairuz will play Zoe, and Mariya Ise will play Ran Midoran. Furthermore, the opening theme song has been decided to be Momoiro Clover Z's "Kaishin Ichigeki."

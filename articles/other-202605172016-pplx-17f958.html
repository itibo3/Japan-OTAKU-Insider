Details (EN)

A collaboration cafe called ``Devil Butler and the Black Cat'' -Royal Rose Tea Party-'' based on the smartphone game ``Devil Butler and the Black Cat'' will be held at Animate Cafe Ikebukuro 3rd store and Nagoya 2nd store. The event will run from July 2nd to July 31st, 2026, and will feature in-game recipe reproduction menus and cafe-only goods for sale.

Details (EN)

Hobby Japan announced today (November 26, 2026) that it will release the Japanese version of the board game “Quark” in late December. The price is 3,740 yen (tax included). It is a party game in which players are divided into two teams: one that uses the roll of the dice as a signal to place a cup over a cork to catch it, and the other that pulls a string to let the cork escape.

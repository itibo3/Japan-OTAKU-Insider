Details (EN)

The “Gundam X Is Always There” project has been launched to commemorate the 30th anniversary of the TV anime *Mobile New Century Gundam X* in 2026. Starting September 14, a total of 39 posters—each inspired by one of the TV series’ 39 episodes—will be displayed at Gundam-related facilities nationwide. Additionally, a giveaway is currently underway on X (formerly Twitter), where 30 winners will be selected at random to receive a special mini-poster.

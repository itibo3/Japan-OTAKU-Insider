Details (EN)

The TV anime *New Oishinbo* is set to air in 2027. It has now been revealed that Yutaka Furusawa, Kosuke Echigoya, Nobuo Tobita, Hiroshi Naka, An Yaoya, and Yuko Ushida will join the cast as members of the Tozai Shimbun, each bringing their own unique personalities to the roles. Furthermore, it has been confirmed that the opening theme song will be “PROMISE ME” by Yui Makino, and the ending theme song will be “Proof” by Skoop On Somebody.

Details (EN)

All 37 episodes of the TV anime *DEATH NOTE* will be broadcast in their entirety for free on ABEMA starting November 5, 2026—the anniversary of L’s death. The series will air nonstop in two blocks: starting at 3:00 p.m. on November 5 and at 9:30 a.m. on November 6, and will also be available for free streaming for one week following the broadcast.

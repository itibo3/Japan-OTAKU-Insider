11bitstudios announced that it will release the Switch version of the survival simulation game “Frostpunk” on October 19. Featuring touch controls and a dedicated UI, this social simulation—set on a Earth frozen solid by a massive cold wave—lets you enjoy the game right in the palm of your hand as you strive to keep your city alive while managing the discontent of people relying on steam engines to survive.

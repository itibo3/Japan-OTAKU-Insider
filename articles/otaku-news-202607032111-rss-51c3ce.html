Details (EN)

A collaboration event with the movie ``The Birth of Kitaro: The Mystery of Gegege'' is currently being held in ``Gegege no Kitaro Yokai Yokocho'', an online game themed around the anime ``GeGeGe no Kitaro''. "Mystery of Yokai Yokocho Gegege ~Mizuki Edition~" has been running since July 2nd, and "Mystery of Yokai Yokocho Gegege ~Kitarou's Father Edition~" will start after maintenance on July 16th.

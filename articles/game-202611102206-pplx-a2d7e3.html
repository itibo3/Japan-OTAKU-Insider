On November 5, 2026, Taito released a free update (Ver. 1.0.15) for “Groove Coaster: Future Performers” on Nintendo Switch. A total of four new tracks—including three popular songs from the series and a 15th-anniversary track—as well as Chapter 11 of Story Mode have been added. Additionally, an Autumn Sale offering 50% off the console is being held through November 16.

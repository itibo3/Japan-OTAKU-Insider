Details (EN)

KADOKAWA announced today that it will hold “PERSONA30thEX.,” a large-scale exhibition commemorating the 30th anniversary of the “Persona” series. The exhibition will take place at the Ueno Royal Museum in Tokyo from January 21 to February 7, 2027. Additionally, further details are scheduled to be announced on the official X account at 7:00 p.m. on October 23.

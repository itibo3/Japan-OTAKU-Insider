Alma Bianca, a retailer of original manga and anime merchandise, will be operating the “AMNIBUS” booth at “Jump Festa 2027.” At the booth, new merchandise for *Haikyū!!* and *Jujutsu Kaisen*, as well as a large number of new, specially drawn items for *SAKAMOTO DAYS*, *Gintama*, and *Forgotten Battery*, will be available for pre-release purchase.

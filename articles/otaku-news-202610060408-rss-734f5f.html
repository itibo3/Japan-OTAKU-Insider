Volume 1 [AA] of *I Was the Eldest Daughter of the Duke’s Family*, a manga adaptation of Sayaka Suzune’s light novel illustrated by Nurupyo Ayakawa, is already on sale in Akihabara. The promotional text for the manga reads, “I’ve abandoned my family and my past—now I’m starting to live my own life!” The obi tagline states, “My life of suppressing my true feelings as the ‘eldest daughter of the duke’s family’ is finally over...”

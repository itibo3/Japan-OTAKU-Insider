Details (EN)

MegaHouse’s top pick at the “TOKYO Toy Show 2026” is the 3D puzzle “KAITANICS Honda Super Cub 110.” It’s a unique product that reimagines Honda’s Super Cub motorcycle within the context of MegaHouse’s popular “Disassembly Puzzle” series. It is scheduled for release in December and will cost 7,700 yen. There are four varieties in total. At the venue, we were able to speak with a company representative about their vision for the product.

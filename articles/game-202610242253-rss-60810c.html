Details (EN)

The monthly series “My Collaboration Café,” published on the fourth Saturday of every month, is a column in which Tetsuya Onishi—a working chef—creates his own collaborations with various games and other sources to introduce recipes inspired by them. This installment’s theme is the ramen shop management simulation “JapaneseRamenSimulator.” Inspired by a monk character who appears in the game, we asked him to create a vegetarian ramen that uses no meat, fish, or the five pungent vegetables.

Osaka Electro-Communication University announced today that ``KeyTyper,'' a student work from the university's Digital Game Department, was exhibited at ``Digital Lab,'' a large game exhibition facility newly established at Ikenotaira Hotel & Resorts in Nagano Prefecture. This work won the ``Ikenotaira Hotel Award'' at ``Game Creator Koshien 2025''.

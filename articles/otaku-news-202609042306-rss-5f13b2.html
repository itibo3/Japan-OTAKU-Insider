To commemorate the 30th anniversary of *Pokémon*, “Pokémon All-Stars 1025” is a massive, approximately 22-minute epic that sings the names of all 1,025 Pokémon. We spoke with Masayoshi Oishi—who wrote the lyrics, composed the music, and performed the song—about his feelings upon receiving this unprecedented offer, behind-the-scenes stories from the production, and his favorite Pokémon. We also bring you coverage from the press conference celebrating the music video’s release, where he appeared on stage alongside Ann Mika and Yusei Yagi.

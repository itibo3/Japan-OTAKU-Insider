Hanayama will release three types of ``Huzzle Disney Villains'', a new product in the company's 3D puzzle ``HUZZLE'' series, on August 29th. As the product name suggests, the theme is "villains" that appear in Disney works. Three-dimensional puzzles are made of icons representing three of the most popular characters: "Jafar," "Queen of Hearts," and "Queen."

Details (EN)

On June 21st, the Takara Tomy official ambassador event ``Torabo CUP'' was held at Nakano's gaming space ``RedBull GamingSphere Tokyo'', hosted by ``Torabo'', a BEYBLADE X official event ambassador and a professional fighting gamer who belongs to the e-sports team ``La VISION''. This event will be held under team rules, with players competing as a team of three. The admission fee is 100 yen, and due to the venue, only participants and related parties can enter this time.

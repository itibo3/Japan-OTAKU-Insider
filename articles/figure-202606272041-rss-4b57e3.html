「ARMORED CORE VI FIRES OF RUBICON」より「AAP07: BALTEUS」が食玩「SMP」で立体化！2027年4月発売予定。プレバンにて予約開始

Summary

Bandai will release the candy toy "SMP [SHOKUGAN MODELING PROJECT] ARMORED CORE VI FIRES OF RUBICON AAP07: BALTEUS" in April 2027. Pre-orders will begin on Premium Bandai from 7pm on June 27th, and the price is 49,940 yen.

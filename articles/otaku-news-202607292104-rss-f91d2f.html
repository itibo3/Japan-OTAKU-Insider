In conjunction with the 20th anniversary of the manga ``FAIRY TAIL'' and the start of serialization of the new series ``FAIRY TAIL RE: FANTASIA'', illustrations contributed by authors from across the boundaries of publishers and editorial departments have been released. George Morikawa from ``Hajime no Ippo'', Yudetamago from ``Kinnikuman'', and Yoshihiro Togashi from ``HUNTER x HUNTER'' drew Natsu and his friends.

Details (EN)

The TV anime *The Seven Knights of the Kingdom of Maronie* will begin airing on NHK E-Tele on October 3, 2026. It has now been announced that the opening theme will be “I Won’t Say Goodbye” by yama, and the second promotional video featuring the song has been released. Furthermore, the second wave of additional cast members—including Miyuki Sakurai, Natsumi Fujiwara, Takehito Koyasu, Kazuyuki Okitsu, and Daisuke Namikawa—has been announced, and comments from them have been released.
